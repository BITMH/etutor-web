Unit 1: Chapter 1: New Year, New You (Nouns & Articles)

Master common and proper nouns, countable and uncountable nouns, and correct use of indefinite articles 'a' and 'an' while exploring New Year celebrations and writing personal resolutions.
Rujukan Rasmi: SK 1.1, 2.1, 3.1, 4.1 (CEFR Form 1 KSSM English)

Nota Ringkas Bahasa Inggeris Tingkatan 1 • Sedia untuk cetakan atau simpanan PDF.

Objektif Pembelajaran DSKP

Sasaran Penguasaan Bab Ini

  1. Distinguish between common nouns and proper nouns accurately in context.
  2. Classify everyday objects and New Year items into countable and uncountable nouns.
  3. Apply indefinite articles 'a' and 'an' based on initial vowel or consonant sounds rather than spelling alone.
  4. Apply Circle Map pre-writing strategies to brainstorm and write New Year resolutions.
Nota Ringkas & Contoh Terkawal (pp. 1–12)

Ringkasan Teras Mengikut Subtopik

1.1 Common Nouns vs. Proper Nouns (4.1.1)

Nouns name people, places, animals, objects and ideas. Common nouns refer to general items, whereas proper nouns name specific individuals, festivals, days or institutions and always begin with a capital letter.

  • Common nouns stay lowercase unless starting a sentence: celebration, uncle, relative, lantern, temple.
  • Proper nouns require initial capitals: New Year's Eve, Deepavali, Hari Raya Aidilfitri, January, Malaysia.
  • Specific festival names and family calendar events in Chapter 1 ('New Year, New You') follow proper noun capitalisation rules.

Contoh Terkawal: Identify two common nouns and two proper nouns in: 'Every January, Vanessa invites her uncle to Kuala Lumpur for a barbecue.'

Penyelesaian: Proper Nouns: January, Vanessa, Kuala Lumpur. Common Nouns: uncle, barbecue.

Tip Penting: Always capitalise months, days of the week, personal names, cities and official festival titles.

1.2 Countable and Uncountable Nouns (4.1.1 iii–iv)

Understanding whether a noun can be counted determines singular/plural verb agreement and whether articles like 'a/an' or quantifiers such as 'many/much' are grammatical.

  • Countable nouns have singular and plural forms: balloon/balloons, prayer/prayers, resolution/resolutions.
  • Uncountable nouns name liquids, powders, materials or abstract concepts without a plural '-s': water, rice, advice, happiness, music.
  • Use 'many' or numerals with countable plural nouns, and 'much' or 'a little' with uncountable nouns.

Contoh Terkawal: Choose the correct form: 'We shared (many / much) useful advice before making three New Year (resolution / resolutions).'

Penyelesaian: 'much' goes with uncountable 'advice' (never 'advices'), and 'resolutions' takes plural '-s' after 'three'.

Tip Penting: Words like 'advice', 'information', 'homework' and 'luggage' are uncountable in Standard English.

1.3 Indefinite Articles 'a' and 'an' by Sound (4.1.4)

Indefinite articles 'a' and 'an' are used before singular countable nouns introduced for the first time. Choosing between 'a' and 'an' depends strictly on the spoken sound at the start of the word.

  • Use 'an' before vowel sounds (/a/, /e/, /i/, /o/, /u/): an annual reunion, an honest resolution, an umbrella.
  • Use 'a' before consonant sounds even if spelled with a vowel letter: a university (starts with /j/ 'yu'), a one-day camp (starts with /w/).
  • Never use 'a' or 'an' before plural nouns or uncountable nouns such as 'advice' or 'celebrations'.

Contoh Terkawal: Fill in 'a' or 'an': 'Joining ___ annual family reunion is ___ honour for everyone.'

Penyelesaian: 'an annual' (vowel sound /æ/) and 'an honour' (silent 'h', starts with vowel sound /ɒ/).

Tip Penting: Say the word aloud first: 'hour' and 'honest' start with vowel sounds so they take 'an'.

1.4 Pre-Writing with a Circle Map & Vocabulary Journal (3.1.3)

Chapter 1 introduces the Circle Map to brainstorm ideas about traditions and goals, alongside keeping a Vocabulary Journal with definitions and original sentences.

  • Write the central topic (e.g., 'My New Year Resolutions') inside the inner circle of the Circle Map.
  • Fill the outer circle with concrete phrases: improve English speaking, join sports training, manage time well.
  • Include frame-of-reference sources such as coach advice, family expectations and reading material.

Contoh Terkawal: Draft a resolution paragraph from a Circle Map containing 'read two English books a month' and 'keep a Vocabulary Journal'.

Penyelesaian: This year, I plan to improve my English by reading two storybooks every month and recording ten new words each week in my Vocabulary Journal.

Tip Penting: Organising ideas visually in a Circle Map prevents writer's block and keeps paragraphs focused.

Amaran Pemeriksa

3 Kesilapan Lazim & Tip Skor Exam

Kesilapan Lazim: Writing 'a hour' or 'an university' by looking only at the first letter.

Pembetulan Tepat: Write 'an hour' (silent 'h') and 'a university' (pronounced 'yoo-ni-ver-si-ty').

Tip Skor Exam: Test by listening to the initial sound, not reading the alphabet letter.

Kesilapan Lazim: Adding '-s' to uncountable nouns like 'informations', 'homeworks' or 'advices'.

Pembetulan Tepat: Keep them singular: 'some information', 'a piece of advice', 'much homework'.

Tip Skor Exam: Use counting phrases like 'two pieces of advice' when quantity matters.

Kesilapan Lazim: Writing festivals or months with lowercase initials such as 'new year' or 'january'.

Pembetulan Tepat: Capitalise proper nouns: 'New Year's Day', 'January'.

Tip Skor Exam: Proofread all dates and celebrations at the end of writing.
