OBJEKTIF PEMBELAJARAN DSKP

01 Use 'be going to' accurately to express planned future intentions and decisions made before speaking.
02 Apply 'will' and 'won't' for spontaneous offers, promises and predictions about money and jobs.
03 Construct polite requests and preferences with 'would like + to-infinitive' in affirmative, negative and question forms.
04 Read and write advice on online message boards about teenagers earning and saving pocket money responsibly.
SUBTOPIK 01

1.1 Earning & Saving Money Vocabulary (Pulse 2 Unit 6)

DSKP Form 2 opens the Pulse 2 textbook syllabus at Unit 6 ('Money'), exploring part-time chores, budgeting, charity fundraising and smart consumer choices for teenagers.

  • Chores & pocket money verbs: babysit, do the washing-up, walk the dog, wash cars, deliver newspapers, clean windows.
  • Money verbs: earn, save, spend (on), borrow (from), lend (to), swap, pay for, waste, collect money for charity.
  • Compare pairs carefully: 'borrow from' means taking temporarily; 'lend to' means giving temporarily.

Contoh: Choose the correct verb: 'Could you (lend / borrow) me RM10? I promise I will pay it back tomorrow.'

Penyelesaian: lend (the speaker asks someone else to give money temporarily; 'Can I borrow RM10 from you?' reverses the subject).

Tip Penting: Remember: Borrow FROM a lender; Lend TO a borrower.

SUBTOPIK 02

1.2 Future Plans with 'be going to'

When talking about concrete plans, budgets or intentions decided BEFORE the moment of speaking, English uses 'am/is/are + going to + base verb'.

  • Affirmative: 'I am going to save half of my pocket money this month.' / 'She is going to wash her neighbour's car.'
  • Negative: 'We aren't going to waste money on expensive snacks.'
  • Questions: 'Are you going to buy a second-hand bicycle?' -> 'Yes, I am.' / 'No, I'm not.'

Contoh: Complete with 'going to': 'Adam saved RM80 last month because he ______ (buy) a new school bag.'

Penyelesaian: is going to buy (a pre-decided plan supported by prior action).

Tip Penting: Always include the right form of 'be' (am/is/are) before 'going to' + base verb.

SUBTOPIK 03

1.3 Future Predictions & Quick Decisions with 'will / won't'

Use 'will' (or contracted ''ll') and 'will not' ('won't') followed by the bare infinitive when making general predictions, promises or spontaneous offers on the spot.

  • Spontaneous offer/decision: 'Oh, you forgot your wallet! I'll pay for your bus ticket today.'
  • General prediction: 'In the future, more shops will accept cashless payments.'
  • Notice: Never add 'to' or '-s' after 'will' or 'won't' ('He will helps' is wrong -> 'He will help').

Contoh: Decide between 'will' and 'am going to': 'Look, my sister is carrying heavy groceries! Wait, I ______ help her.'

Penyelesaian: 'will / 'll' (a spontaneous decision made right at the moment of speaking).

Tip Penting: Pre-planned before now = 'going to'; Instant reaction right now = 'will'.

SUBTOPIK 04

1.4 Polite Preferences: 'would like + infinitive' (DSKP p. 50)

DSKP Form 2 explicitly specifies 'would like + infinitive (to + verb)' for stating wishes politely and answering Yes/No questions cleanly.

  • Affirmative & Negative: 'Jess would like to earn extra money.' / 'Jess wouldn't like to babysit noisy toddlers.'
  • Questions & Short Answers: 'Would you like to go shopping with us?' -> 'Yes, I would.' / 'No, I wouldn't.'
  • Contrast 'would like to + verb' (polite specific wish) with 'like + verb-ing' (general hobby: 'I like saving coins').

Contoh: Respond politely in short form to: 'Would you like to volunteer at the charity book fair?'

Penyelesaian: 'Yes, I would.' (Avoid saying 'Yes, I would like.' as the standalone short answer; either 'Yes, I would.' or 'Yes, I'd love to.').

Tip Penting: DSKP target rule: 'Yes, I would.' / 'No, I wouldn't.'

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #1

ELAKKAN: Writing 'I would like going shopping' instead of 'to go shopping'.

TEPAT: Always follow 'would like' with 'to + infinitive': 'I would like to go shopping.'

Tip Skor Exam: 'Would like' wants 'to'; plain 'like' often takes '-ing'.

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #2

ELAKKAN: Mixing up 'borrow' and 'lend': 'Can you borrow me your pen?'

TEPAT: Say 'Can you lend me your pen?' or 'Can I borrow your pen?'

Tip Skor Exam: You lend OUT; you borrow IN.

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #3

ELAKKAN: Dropping 'be' in 'be going to', e.g., 'She going to save money.'

TEPAT: Include am/is/are: 'She is going to save money.'

Tip Skor Exam: Three parts required: BE + GOING TO + BASE VERB.

Huraian: The negative form in DSKP Form 2 is 'wouldn't like + to-infinitive': 'Jess wouldn't like to babysit'.

Soalan 2 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the natural short reply to 'Would you like to join our recycling sale?'

Huraian: DSKP specifies 'Yes, I would.' / 'No, I wouldn't.' for 'Would you like...' questions.

Soalan 3 / 20 +10 XP

Which verb completes this sentence accurately: 'Aina wants to ______ RM50 from her elder brother until Friday.'

Huraian: 'Borrow ... from' means receiving something temporarily with a promise to return it.

Soalan 4 / 20 +10 XP

Look at the dark clouds and strong wind! It ______ soon.

Huraian: When clear present evidence points to an immediate event, use 'is going to'.

Soalan 5 / 20 +10 XP

'Wait, I haven't got enough coins for the vending machine.' — 'Don't worry, I ______ buy a bottle of water for you.'

Huraian: Spontaneous offers made at the moment of speaking take 'will' ('I'll buy...').

Soalan 6 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence expresses a pre-planned intention correctly?

Huraian: 'Be going to' requires plural 'are' before 'going to + base verb'.

Soalan 7 / 20 +10 XP

On an online message board for teens, which advice shows smart money management?

Huraian: Unit 6 message-board texts teach comparing prices, budgeting and saving regularly.

Soalan 8 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the grammatically correct negative prediction.

Huraian: Modal 'won't' is always followed directly by the base verb ('disappear').

Soalan 9 / 20 +10 XP

What does 'swap' mean in teen money lessons?

Huraian: Swapping books or clothes saves money by exchanging items directly with friends.

Soalan 10 / 20 +10 XP

Identify the sentence with ZERO grammatical errors.

Huraian: 'No, I wouldn't.' is grammatically accurate; the others have verb-form or agreement errors.

Soalan 11 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the correct sentence using 'lend' or 'borrow'.

Huraian: You 'lend' something to someone (give temporarily) and 'borrow' something from someone (take temporarily).

Soalan 12 / 20 +10 XP

Which chore involves looking after young children while their parents are away?

Huraian: 'Babysitting' means taking care of a baby or young child for a short time while the parents are out.

Soalan 13 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the dialogue: '______ your brother going to wash the neighbour's car this Saturday?' — 'Yes, he ______.'

Huraian: Singular subject 'your brother' takes 'Is' in a 'be going to' question, and the short answer repeats 'is'.

Soalan 14 / 20 +10 XP

Read the situation: Farah has already bought flour, butter and sugar because she planned a charity bake sale. Which sentence accurately describes her plan?

Huraian: Buying ingredients beforehand shows a prior plan decided before speaking, which requires 'is going to + base verb'.

Soalan 15 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence expresses a promise about borrowed money?

Huraian: Promises made to reassure someone use 'will' ('ll) followed by the bare infinitive 'pay'.

Soalan 16 / 20 +10 XP

Select the grammatically accurate sentence expressing a polite wish.

Huraian: 'Would like' is always followed by 'to + base verb' ('to open'), and modal 'would' never takes '-s'.

Soalan 17 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the best preposition and verb pair: 'Teenagers should ______ part of their allowance instead of ______ it all ______ trendy snacks.'

Huraian: We 'save' money for future use and 'spend' money 'on' items.

Soalan 18 / 20 +10 XP

Haziq earns RM40 a week from walking his neighbour's dog and cleaning windows. He spends RM25 on lunches and saves the rest. How many weeks will it take him to save RM120 for a badminton racket?

Huraian: Haziq saves RM40 - RM25 = RM15 per week. RM120 / RM15 = 8 weeks.

Soalan 19 / 20 +10 XP

Which negative sentence is correctly formed?

Huraian: Plural subject 'They' takes 'aren't' before 'going to + base verb'.

Soalan 20 / 20 +10 XP

Identify the sentence where BOTH parts are grammatically correct.

Huraian: General hobbies take 'like + -ing' ('like collecting'), whereas specific polite wishes take 'would like + to-infinitive' ('would like to visit').
