Sasaran Penguasaan Bab Ini

Nota Ringkas & Contoh Terkawal (DSKP m.s. 51 / Unit 7)

Ringkasan Teras Mengikut Subtopik

2.1 Present Perfect Tense: Form & Irregular Participles

The Present Perfect links past experiences or ongoing situations to the present using 'have' (I/you/we/they) or 'has' (he/she/it) followed by the Past Participle (V3).

  • Regular verbs add '-ed': travelled, visited, booked, stayed.
  • Common travel irregular past participles: go → gone/been, see → seen, ride → ridden, fly → flown, take → taken, write → written.
  • Use 'has been to' when someone visited and returned ('She has been to Langkawi twice'); use 'has gone to' when they are still away there.

Contoh Terkawal: Complete: 'My uncle ______ (fly) across the Pacific Ocean, and my cousins ______ (never / take) a sleeper train.'

Penyelesaian: 'has flown' (singular 'my uncle', V3 of fly = flown) and 'have never taken' (plural 'cousins', V3 of take = taken).

Tip Penting: Never confuse the Past Simple ('flew', 'took') with the Past Participle ('flown', 'taken') after 'have/has'.

2.2 'Ever' & 'Never' for Life Experiences

When asking or talking about experiences in someone's whole life up to now (when the exact date is not important), English uses 'ever' and 'never' with the Present Perfect.

  • Question with 'ever': 'Have you ever ridden a ferry across Penang Strait?' / 'Has she ever climbed Mount Kinabalu?'
  • Negative with 'never' (use a positive 'have/has'): 'I have never missed a flight.' (Do NOT write 'haven't never').
  • Follow-up details with a specific past time switch back to Past Simple: 'Yes, I have! I rode it last December.'

Contoh Terkawal: Fix the double-negative error: 'We haven't never travelled by helicopter before.'

Penyelesaian: We have never travelled by helicopter before. (Or: 'We haven't ever travelled...')

Tip Penting: Use 'never' with affirmative 'have/has' — never stack 'not' + 'never' together.

2.3 Duration vs. Starting Point: 'for' & 'since' (DSKP p. 51)

Both 'for' and 'since' connect an action that started in the past to the present moment, and DSKP Form 2 specifically highlights this pair for Present Perfect mastery.

  • 'for' + period/length of time: for two hours, for five days, for three years, for a long time.
  • 'since' + exact starting point/date/event: since 8:00 a.m., since Monday, since 2019, since we arrived in Sabah.
  • Question trigger: 'How long have you lived in Johor Bahru?' -> 'I have lived here for six years / since 2018.'

Contoh Terkawal: Fill in 'for' or 'since': 'The train has waited at the station ______ twenty minutes, ever ______ 4:15 p.m.'

Penyelesaian: 'for twenty minutes' (duration) and 'since 4:15 p.m.' (specific starting clock time).

Tip Penting: Ask: Is it a total amount on a stopwatch (FOR) or a mark on a clock/calendar (SINCE)?

2.4 Transport & Journey Verbs (Unit 7 Vocabulary)

Describing journeys smoothly requires choosing accurate transport verbs and prepositions across land, sea and air travel.

  • Preposition by mode: 'by bus', 'by train', 'by plane', 'by ferry', BUT always 'on foot' (never 'by foot').
  • Boarding large public transport (bus, train, plane, ship): 'get on' / 'get off'. Small private vehicles (car, taxi): 'get into' / 'get out of'.
  • Take off (plane leaves ground) vs. land (arrives on ground); set off (begin a journey); check in (at airport/hotel).

Contoh Terkawal: Correct two preposition errors: 'Last Sunday we travelled on train, walked by foot to the jetty and got in the ferry.'

Penyelesaian: Last Sunday we travelled by train, walked on foot to the jetty and got on the ferry.

Tip Penting: Memorise three golden travel prepositions: 'by train', 'on foot', 'get on the bus/ferry'.

Amaran Pemeriksa

3 Kesilapan Lazim & Tip Skor Exam

Kesilapan Lazim: Saying 'I am living here since 2020' instead of using Present Perfect.

Pembetulan Tepat: Use Present Perfect for an unfinished time span until now: 'I have lived here since 2020.'

Tip Skor Exam: 'Since' and 'for (up to now)' trigger 'have/has + V3', not Present Simple/Continuous.

Kesilapan Lazim: Using Past Simple with 'for' improperly or mixing 'for' and 'since' ('since three years').

Pembetulan Tepat: Say 'for three years' (duration) or 'since 2023' (starting year).

Tip Skor Exam: Never write 'since + number of hours/days/years'.

Kesilapan Lazim: Using Past Simple verb V2 after 'has/have', e.g., 'He has went to Kuching.'

Pembetulan Tepat: Use the Past Participle V3: 'He has gone to Kuching' or 'He has been to Kuching'.

Tip Skor Exam: Go - Went - Gone; See - Saw - Seen; Write - Wrote - Written.

Huraian: 9:30 a.m. is a specific starting point in time, so it requires 'since'.

Soalan 2 / 10 +10 XP

Which question accurately asks about a travel experience?

Huraian: 'Have + subject + ever + past participle ('ridden')' is the correct Present Perfect question pattern.

Soalan 3 / 10 +10 XP

Choose the sentence that uses 'for' correctly with the Present Perfect.

Huraian: 'Six years' is a duration of time, paired with 'for' and Present Perfect 'has worked'.

Soalan 4 / 10 +10 XP

Select the grammatically correct travel preposition sentence.

Huraian: Walking uses 'on foot'; cars use 'by car / get into', whereas trains use 'by train / get on'.

Soalan 5 / 10 +10 XP

'Where is Farhan?' — 'He isn't in class right now; he ______ to the school library to borrow an atlas.'

Huraian: 'Has gone to' indicates Farhan is currently at the library and has not returned yet.

Soalan 6 / 10 +10 XP

Fix the verb form: 'Have your parents ______ the tickets for the Langkawi ferry yet?'

Huraian: The past participle of 'buy' is 'bought'.

Soalan 7 / 10 +10 XP

Which negative sentence using 'never' is grammatically accurate?

Huraian: Singular subject 'Lina' takes 'has' + 'never' + past participle 'seen' without adding 'not'.

Soalan 8 / 10 +10 XP

Choose the right meaning for 'set off' in a travel diary: 'We set off at dawn to beat the holiday traffic.'

Huraian: 'Set off' is a common journey phrasal verb meaning to begin a trip.

Soalan 9 / 10 +10 XP

Read the exchange: 'Have you ever been to Sabah?' — 'Yes, I have. I ______ Mount Kinabalu Park two years ago.'

Huraian: When giving a finished past time expression ('two years ago'), switch from Present Perfect to Past Simple ('visited').

Soalan 10 / 10 +10 XP

Which word is the correct Past Participle of 'take'?

Huraian: The principal parts are take (V1) - took (V2) - taken (V3).
