Ringkasan Teras Mengikut Subtopik

SUBTOPIK 01

2.1 Present Perfect Continuous vs. Simple (DSKP Items 7–8)

DSKP Form 3 highlights the contrast between continuous ongoing effort ('have/has been + -ing') and completed achievement ('have/has + V3').

  • Present Perfect Continuous focuses on the ACTIVITY and DURATION starting in the past and continuing now: 'Volunteers have been planting mangroves all morning (and are still tired/working).'
  • Present Perfect Simple focuses on the COMPLETED RESULT or QUANTITY: 'They have planted 80 mangrove saplings today.'
  • Remember: Stative verbs ('know', 'believe', 'like', 'own') NEVER take continuous '-ing' ('I have known her for five years', not 'have been knowing').

Contoh Compare: (A) 'Faris has been cleaning the beach since 8 a.m.' vs (B) 'Faris has collected three bags of plastic.'

Penyelesaian (A) emphasises ongoing duration/activity since 8 a.m. (Continuous); (B) highlights the finished measurable quantity 'three bags' (Simple).

Tip Penting How long / still busy = Continuous ('have been + -ing'); How many / finished = Simple ('have + V3').

SUBTOPIK 02

2.2 Four Future Forms Compared (DSKP Item 9)

English expresses the future differently depending on whether an event is a scheduled timetable, an arranged diary appointment, an intention or an instant promise.

  • 1. Present Simple: official timetables & schedules ('The eco-bus leaves at 7:30 a.m. sharp').
  • 2. Present Continuous: fixed personal arrangements with date/time/others ('We are meeting the park ranger at 9:00 a.m. tomorrow').
  • 3. Going to: prior personal plans or visible evidence ('The river is overflowing — it is going to flood').
  • 4. Will / Won't: promises, predictions without physical proof and instant decisions ('I promise we won't drop litter').

Contoh Pick the best future form: 'Look at our printed camp itinerary! The recycling workshop ______ (start) at 2:15 p.m.'

Penyelesaian starts (Present Simple for fixed official timetable/itinerary).

Tip Penting Train/bus/flight/class schedule = Present Simple! Arranged diary meeting = Present Continuous!

SUBTOPIK 03

2.3 Passive Voice Across Four Tenses (DSKP Item 10)

DSKP Item 10 requires students to form the Passive Voice ('be + Past Participle') across Present Simple, Past Simple, Present Continuous and Present Perfect — ideal for environmental processes and formal reports.

  • Present Simple Passive: 'Plastic bottles ARE RECYCLED here.' | Past Simple Passive: 'Solar panels WERE INSTALLED last year.'
  • Present Continuous Passive: 'The river IS BEING CLEANED right now.' (always keep 'being' + V3!).
  • Present Perfect Passive: 'Ten new bins HAVE BEEN PLACED near the canteen.' (always keep 'been' + V3!).

Contoh Rewrite in Passive Voice: 'The local council is building a new cycling lane now.'

Penyelesaian A new cycling lane is being built by the local council now.

Tip Penting Spot the 'be' ingredient carefully: Continuous keeps 'being'; Perfect keeps 'been'.

SUBTOPIK 04

2.4 Modals of Obligation, Prohibition & Advice

Recycled and consolidated in Form 3 DSKP, modal verbs help students articulate safety rules, healthy living habits and environmental regulations clearly.

  • 'must' (strong personal/rule obligation) vs 'have to' (external rule/duty): 'Students must wear lab goggles.'
  • CRITICAL DISTINCTION: 'mustn't' means PROHIBITED / NOT ALLOWED ('You mustn't burn plastic waste'), whereas 'don't have to' means NOT NECESSARY / OPTIONAL ('You don't have to bring extra water; bottles are provided').
  • Advice & past obligation: 'should / shouldn't' give helpful advice ('You should sleep eight hours'), while past obligation uses 'had to' (never 'musted').

Contoh Explain the difference: 'You mustn't enter the clinic without a mask' vs 'You don't have to pay for the check-up.'

Penyelesaian 'mustn't' strictly forbids entering without a mask; 'don't have to' tells you the check-up is free so payment is unnecessary.

Tip Penting Mustn't = BANNED! Don't have to = OPTIONAL!

Amaran Pemeriksa

3 Kesilapan Lazim & Tip Skor Exam

Elakkan Using 'mustn't' when meaning 'it is not necessary', e.g. 'Tomorrow is Sunday, so we mustn't wear school uniform.'

Tepat Say 'We don't have to wear school uniform on Sunday' (optional/not required, not forbidden!).

Tip Skor Exam: Ask: Will I be punished if I do it? Yes = mustn't; No = don't have to.

Elakkan Dropping 'being' or 'been' in passive sentences ('The trees are planting' or 'The park has cleaned').

Tepat Keep the right passive participle: 'The trees are being planted' (Continuous) or 'The park has been cleaned' (Perfect).

Tip Skor Exam: Check whether the subject receives the action! If a park cannot clean itself, you MUST have 'be + V3'.

Elakkan Using Present Perfect Continuous with stative verbs like 'know' or 'own' ('I have been knowing him').

Tepat Use Present Perfect Simple for stative verbs: 'I have known him since Form 1.'

Tip Skor Exam: State verbs describe existence/mind/feelings, not ongoing physical activity.

Soalan 1 / 20 +10 XP

Complete cleanly: 'Look! My hands are covered in mud because I ______ trees in the school garden for two hours.'

Huraian: Visible current evidence ('hands covered in mud') + ongoing duration ('for two hours') fits Present Perfect Continuous.

Soalan 2 / 20 +10 XP

Contrast with result: 'So far, our eco-team ______ twenty-four saplings near the sports field.'

Huraian: When highlighting a completed quantity ('twenty-four saplings') up to now, use Present Perfect Simple.

Soalan 3 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence uses the correct modal to show something is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN?

Huraian: 'Mustn't' expresses prohibition; 'don't have to' only means not necessary.

Soalan 4 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the correct Passive form in Present Continuous: 'Right now, dirty drains ______ by municipal workers.'

Huraian: Present Continuous Passive follows the structure 'is/are + being + Past Participle (cleaned)'.

Soalan 5 / 20 +10 XP

Which future form fits an official printed train schedule: 'The express train to Ipoh ______ at 8:05 a.m. daily.'?

Huraian: Timetables and public transport schedules use the Simple Present Tense.

Soalan 6 / 20 +10 XP

Select the accurate Present Perfect Passive sentence.

Huraian: Present Perfect Passive requires 'have/has + been + Past Participle'.

Soalan 7 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the correct sentence using a stative verb with 'for'.

Huraian: 'Know' is a stative verb, so it cannot take '-ing' in Present Perfect Continuous.

Soalan 8 / 20 +10 XP

'Wait, I can't join cycling this Saturday; I ______ my dentist at 10 a.m. — I booked the slot last week.'

Huraian: Fixed personal appointments with a scheduled time and other people use Present Continuous for the future.

Soalan 9 / 20 +10 XP

Which modal phrase expresses a past rule correctly?

Huraian: 'Must' has no past tense form; past obligation is expressed with 'had to + base verb'.

Soalan 10 / 20 +10 XP

'Free entry! Visitors ______ buy tickets to attend the Health & Eco Exhibition.' Choose the exact modal.

Huraian: 'Free entry' means buying tickets is unnecessary (optional), expressed by 'don't have to'.

Soalan 11 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the best future form based on visible present evidence: 'Look at those thick black clouds rising from the forest; the haze ______ worse soon.'

Huraian: 'Be going to' is used for predictions supported by clear visible evidence in the present.

Soalan 12 / 20 +10 XP

'Oh no, plastic bottles are floating in the drain!' — 'Don't worry, I ______ a pair of tongs right now and clear them.'

Huraian: 'Will' expresses a spontaneous decision or offer made at the exact moment of speaking.

Soalan 13 / 20 +10 XP

Rewrite in Passive Voice: 'The municipal council installed twenty solar-powered lamps along the park trail last month.'

Huraian: Active Past Simple ('installed') converts to Past Simple Passive ('were installed') because of the plural subject and finished time 'last month'.

Soalan 14 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the pair that contrasts ongoing duration with completed quantity: 'Sarah ______ the eco-camp report since 9 a.m., and she ______ four pages so far.'

Huraian: Ongoing activity/duration ('since 9 a.m.') uses Present Perfect Continuous ('has been writing'), while completed quantity ('four pages') uses Present Perfect Simple ('has completed').

Soalan 15 / 20 +10 XP

Your friend has sore eyes from using a phone in the dark. Which piece of modal advice is grammatically accurate?

Huraian: Modals 'should' and 'shouldn't' are always followed directly by the base verb (bare infinitive) without 'to', '-ing' or '-ed'.

Soalan 16 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the lab notice: 'Participants ______ bring their own safety gloves because gloves are provided, but they ______ touch the metal shredder.'

Huraian: 'Don't have to' means bringing gloves is unnecessary, while 'mustn't' strictly forbids touching the dangerous shredder.

Soalan 17 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the recycling process in Present Simple Passive: 'At this plant, used aluminium cans ______ into blocks before they are melted.'

Huraian: Regular processes use Present Simple Passive: plural 'cans' + 'are' + past participle ('crushed').

Soalan 18 / 20 +10 XP

Spot the sentence that contains a Passive Voice error.

Huraian: In Present Perfect Passive, the main verb must be the past participle ('have been restored'), not the '-ing' form ('restoring').

Soalan 19 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the sentence that expresses a strong health rule accurately.

Huraian: 'Must' is followed directly by the bare infinitive ('wash') without 'to' or '-ing'.

Soalan 20 / 20 +10 XP

Which future form fits a confirmed diary arrangement? 'I have already bought the bus tickets and booked the guide—we ______ Taman Negara at 7 a.m. this Saturday.'

Huraian: Pre-arranged personal plans with tickets booked and a specific time/place use the Present Continuous ('are visiting') for the future.
