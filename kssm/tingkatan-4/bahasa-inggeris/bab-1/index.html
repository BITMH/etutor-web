OBJEKTIF PEMBELAJARAN DSKP

01 Contrast Present Simple (permanent facts, habits) with Present Continuous (actions in progress, temporary changes) and identify stative verbs that resist continuous '-ing'.
02 Form accurate comparative, superlative and 'as ... as' / 'not as ... as' structures comparing modern messaging with letters or earlier mobile phones.
03 Apply 'used to + base verb' (past habits/states no longer true) and distinguish it from 'be/get used to + noun/-ing' (familiarity).
04 Form nouns ending in '-ion', '-ation' and '-ment' (word building) and use ICT verbs ('scroll', 'paste', 'delete', 'crash', 'print') accurately.
SUBTOPIK 01

1.1 Present Simple vs. Present Continuous & Stative Verbs

Module 1 opens with how technology changes the way we keep in touch. Choosing between Present Simple and Present Continuous depends on whether a situation is permanent or temporary/in progress, and whether the verb is a stative verb.

  • Present Simple: habits, routines, permanent states ('Young people hardly ever send paper letters'; 'Social media connects families worldwide').
  • Present Continuous: actions happening right now or temporary changing trends ('More teens are using voice notes nowadays'; 'She is typing a reply right now').
  • Stative Verbs (see, like, want, seem, understand, need, believe, imagine, know, cost, own): normally NOT used in Present Continuous even when true now ('I understand you', NEVER 'I am understanding'). Watch sense/action pairs like 'think' (opinion = stative) vs 'thinking about' (mental action).

Contoh: Choose the right tense: 'Amir usually ______ (check) his messages after dinner, but tonight he ______ (study) offline so he ______ (need) quiet.'

Penyelesaian: 'checks' (routine), 'is studying' (temporary action tonight), 'needs' (stative verb — no '-ing').

Tip Penting: Always check if the verb expresses a state of mind/feeling/possession before adding '-ing'!

SUBTOPIK 02

1.2 Comparisons: Comparatives, Superlatives & 'as...as'

When comparing old phones like Martin Cooper's 1973/1983 Motorola DynaTAC ('From Brick to Smartphone', p. 11) with modern devices, accurate comparison structures are essential.

  • One-syllable adjectives take '-er / -est' (lighter, the fastest); multi-syllable adjectives take 'more/less ... than' and 'the most/least ...' ('more convenient than', 'the most popular'). Irregular: good–better–best, bad–worse–worst, far–farther/further.
  • Equality & inequality: 'as + adjective + as' ('Sending emails isn't as popular as instant messaging today'). Note: NEVER use comparative '-er/more' inside 'as ___ as'!
  • Modifiers before comparatives add nuance: 'much / far / a lot easier', 'a bit / slightly cheaper'.

Contoh: Complete using comparison: 'In 1983, DynaTAC phones were ______ (heavy) and ______ (expensive) than today's smartphones, and batteries weren't as ______ (reliable) as now.'

Penyelesaian: 'heavier', 'more expensive', 'reliable' (plain base adjective between 'as ... as').

Tip Penting: Remember: 'as + BASE ADJECTIVE + as' — never 'as heavier as' or 'as more popular as'!

SUBTOPIK 03

1.3 Past Habits ('used to') vs. Adaptation ('be/get used to')

Module 1 tests both talking about discontinued past habits ('used to') and getting comfortable with new technology ('be/get used to').

  • 'used to + BASE VERB' (only past! No present 'use to' for current habits): 'People used to carry coins for payphones, but they didn't use to stream videos.' / 'Did your parents use to write letters?' (Drop '-d' after 'did/didn't'!).
  • 'be used to + NOUN / VERB-ING' = be accustomed/comfortable with now: 'Students are used to attending online quizzes now.'
  • 'get used to + NOUN / VERB-ING' = gradually becoming comfortable: 'Grandpa soon got used to making video calls.'

Contoh: Spot the difference: (A) 'I used to read print newspapers.' vs (B) 'I am used to reading e-books on a tablet.'

Penyelesaian: (A) Past habit that stopped ('used to' + base verb 'read'). (B) Present familiarity ('be used to' + '-ing' 'reading').

Tip Penting: No 'be/get' = past habit + V1; Has 'be/get' = familiarity + V-ing/Noun!

SUBTOPIK 04

1.4 ICT Vocabulary & Word Building (-ion, -ation, -ment)

Module 1 strengthens SPM essay and Part 2 vocabulary by transforming verbs into abstract nouns and mastering digital collocations.

  • Word-building nouns: communicate → communication, inform → information, imagine → imagination, educate → education, connect → connection, entertain → entertainment, improve → improvement, equip → equipment.
  • Digital collocations (p. 10): scroll down a page, click an icon, paste text, save a document, crash unexpectedly, delete spam, print a draft.
  • Informal blog/profile writing: group details into clear categories (Interests, Daily Routine, Contact Preferences) and check tenses consistently.

Contoh: Convert the bracketed verbs into nouns: 'Video calling brings huge ______ (improve) to global ______ (communicate).'

Penyelesaian: 'improvement' and 'communication'.

Tip Penting: Notice spelling shifts when adding '-ation' or '-ion' (drop silent '-e' in 'communicate → communication').

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #1

ELAKKAN: Keeping '-d' on 'used to' in questions and negatives: 'Did he used to have a Nokia phone?' or 'I didn't used to chat online.'

TEPAT: Drop '-d' after 'did' / 'didn't': 'Did he use to have...?' and 'I didn't use to chat...'.

Tip Skor Exam: Just like any regular past verb after 'did', 'used' reverts to base 'use'!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #2

ELAKKAN: Using a base verb after 'be/get used to': 'She is used to type fast on mobile.'

TEPAT: After 'be/get used to', 'to' is a preposition, so follow with '-ing' or a noun: 'She is used to typing fast.'

Tip Skor Exam: 'be used to' + -ING / noun!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #3

ELAKKAN: Using 'more' or '-er' inside 'as ... as' ('Emails aren't as faster as chats').

TEPAT: Use the positive base adjective inside 'as ... as': 'Emails aren't as fast as chats.'

Tip Skor Exam: 'as + plain adjective + as'.

Soalan 1 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the correct verb forms: 'Right now, Laila ______ (upload) photos, though she ______ (prefer) face-to-face chats with friends.'

Huraian: 'Right now' triggers Present Continuous ('is uploading'), whereas 'prefer' is a stative verb taking Present Simple ('prefers').

Soalan 2 / 20 +10 XP

Complete accurately: 'Texting is ______ than posting a physical letter across the country.'

Huraian: Use 'much more + long adjective + than'; 'more easier' is a double comparative error.

Soalan 3 / 20 +10 XP

Select the sentence with ZERO grammatical errors using 'used to'.

Huraian: 'Did + subject + use to + V1' is correct; Option B keeps '-d' after didn't, C misuses 'use to' for a present habit, D needs 'waking'.

Soalan 4 / 20 +10 XP

Fill in the gap: 'When my laptop ______ yesterday, I panicked, luckily I had ______ my Science notes five minutes earlier.'

Huraian: Tech collocations from p. 10: computers 'crash' and files are 'saved'.

Soalan 5 / 20 +10 XP

Which word-building transformation is spelled CORRECTLY?

Huraian: 'entertain + -ment = entertainment'; the others are information, communication and equipment.

Soalan 6 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the 'be/get used to' sentence: 'At first online classes felt strange, but Maya soon got used to ______ notes digitally.'

Huraian: 'get used to' is followed by a gerund (-ing) or noun: 'got used to taking'.

Soalan 7 / 20 +10 XP

Which statement is true about Martin Cooper's DynaTAC phone from Module 1 ('From Brick to Smartphone')?

Huraian: Module 1 p. 11 explains Martin Cooper invented the DynaTAC in 1973 (~1 kg brick) and it went on sale in 1983.

Soalan 8 / 20 +10 XP

Fix the comparison: 'Modern voice messages aren't as ______ (formal) than printed business letters.'

Huraian: Always pair 'as' with 'as' (never 'than') around the plain adjective: 'as formal as'.

Soalan 9 / 20 +10 XP

In which sentence does 'think' act as an action verb rather than a stative verb?

Huraian: When 'think' means 'consider/plan' (mental activity), continuous 'are thinking' is natural; when meaning 'have an opinion', it is stative.

Soalan 10 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the most accurate noun form: 'Reliable Internet ______ (connect) makes virtual teamwork smooth.'

Huraian: 'Connect' forms the noun 'connection' (-ion).

Soalan 11 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the correct pair of tenses: 'My sister ______ (hardly ever / post) selfies online, but this week she ______ (share) photos from her Penang trip.'

Huraian: Frequency habits ('hardly ever') take Present Simple ('posts'), while temporary current periods ('this week') take Present Continuous ('is sharing').

Soalan 12 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence uses a stative verb INCORRECTLY?

Huraian: 'Own' expresses possession and is a stative verb, so it should be Present Simple ('Amir owns two external hard drives'), not continuous.

Soalan 13 / 20 +10 XP

Select the grammatically accurate comparison: 'With fibre broadband, downloading large files is ______ than it was ten years ago.'

Huraian: Use a modifier like 'a lot' + '-er' comparative ('a lot faster than'). 'More faster' is a double comparative and 'the fastest' is superlative.

Soalan 14 / 20 +10 XP

Rewrite accurately without changing meaning: 'Typed emails are less personal than handwritten postcards.'

Huraian: 'Less + adj + than' equals 'not as + base adj + as' ('aren't as personal as').

Soalan 15 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the paragraph accurately: 'Years ago, travellers ______ carry paper maps, so older drivers needed time to ______ GPS navigation apps.'

Huraian: 'Used to + base verb' describes a past habit ('used to carry'), while 'get used to + noun' means gradually adapting to something new ('get used to GPS navigation apps').

Soalan 16 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence is grammatically CORRECT?

Huraian: Negative past habit drops the '-d' after 'didn't' ('didn't use to enjoy'). Options A and D need gerunds ('taking', 'waking'), and B keeps '-d' after 'Did'.

Soalan 17 / 20 +10 XP

After copying the paragraph from your notes, which ICT action places it into the new email draft?

Huraian: In Module 1 ICT vocabulary, 'paste' inserts copied text into another document or message.

Soalan 18 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the sentence where BOTH derived nouns are spelled correctly.

Huraian: 'Imagination' (imagine + -ation) and 'improvement' (improve + -ment) are accurate noun forms; the other options contain incorrect suffixes.

Soalan 19 / 20 +10 XP

When writing an informal personal profile or blog post for Module 1, which feature makes your writing clear and engaging?

Huraian: Module 1 writing guidelines emphasise organising personal profiles into logical sections (Interests, Daily Routine, Contact) using friendly, accurate tenses.

Soalan 20 / 20 +10 XP

In which sentence is 'see' used in a DYNAMIC (non-stative) sense meaning 'meet someone by appointment'?

Huraian: When 'see' means 'have an appointment/meeting with', Present Continuous ('am seeing') is standard; when referring to eyesight or understanding, it is stative.
