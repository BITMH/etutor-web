OBJEKTIF PEMBELAJARAN DSKP

01 Sequence earlier past events and ongoing duration before a past turning point using Past Perfect Simple ('had + V3') and Past Perfect Continuous ('had been + V-ing').
02 Convert direct statements into Reported Speech accurately with tense backshifts, 'say' vs 'tell', modal changes and time/place shifts.
03 Report Wh-questions, Yes/No questions ('if/whether'), commands ('told/warned + obj + not to V1') and reporting verbs ('suggest + V-ing', 'apologise for + V-ing').
04 Upgrade weak 'very + adjective' pairs to strong adjectives ('absolutely exhausted/freezing') and address every prompt note in travel emails.
SUBTOPIK 01

5.1 Past Perfect Simple vs. Past Perfect Continuous

When narrating journeys, flight delays, or mountain adventures, Past Perfect clarifies which action happened earlier and how long an effort lasted.

  • Past Perfect Simple ('had + V3'): action completed BEFORE another past action or time ('By the time we reached Gate 15, the plane had already departed').
  • Past Perfect Continuous ('had been + V-ing'): stresses DURATION up to a past moment ('It had been snowing heavily for three days before the sky cleared') or visible past results ('She was exhausted because she had been hiking').
  • Travel agent/airport lexis (p. 76 & p. 144): 'set off' (begin a journey), 'boarding' (getting on a plane), 'on arrival' (immediately upon arriving), 'not a piece of cake' (difficult), explorer, survivor.

Contoh: Complete: 'We ______ (talk) about visiting Japan for years before we finally ______ (book) our tickets last June.'

Penyelesaian: 'had been talking' (multi-year duration before the past decision) and 'booked' (Past Simple second event).

Tip Penting: 'Earlier completed event' = had + V3; 'Duration before past moment' = had been + V-ing!

SUBTOPIK 02

5.2 Reported Statements, Backshifts & Time/Place Changes

Converting travellers' quotes into reported speech requires coordinated updates to reporting verbs, pronouns, tenses, modals, and adverbs.

  • 'say' vs 'tell': 'Irina said (that)...' (no indirect object) vs 'She told Tom (that)...' (MUST take a person object).
  • Tense & Modal Backshift: Present Simple -> Past Simple; Present Continuous -> Past Continuous; Past Simple / Present Perfect -> Past Perfect ('had + V3'); 'will -> would', 'can -> could', 'may -> might', 'must -> had to'. ('could/should/would/might/used to' do NOT change!).
  • Time & Place shifts: 'now -> then', 'here -> there', 'today -> that day', 'yesterday -> the day before / previous day', 'tomorrow -> the next / following day', 'ago -> before'.

Contoh: Report: 'I saw a wild bear here yesterday,' said Lars.

Penyelesaian: Lars said (that) he had seen a wild bear there the day before / the previous day.

Tip Penting: Always shift ALL four elements together: pronoun, verb tense, location ('here->there'), and time ('yesterday->the day before')!

SUBTOPIK 03

5.3 Reported Questions, Commands & Reporting Verb Patterns

Module 5 tests indirect question word order (no 'do/did' and no question mark!) plus exact verb patterns after verbs like 'suggest', 'warn', and 'apologise'.

  • Reported Wh- and Yes/No Questions: keep statement order (Subject + Verb) with NO 'do/does/did' and end with a full stop '.'! Use 'if / whether' for Yes/No questions ('She asked me if I knew anything about Pompeii'; 'He asked where I was flying').
  • Reported Commands & Requests: 'told / asked / warned + object + (not) to + V1' ('The guide warned us not to leave the marked trail').
  • Reporting Verbs: '+ V-ing' (suggest, deny, admit, apologise FOR: 'Ryan suggested going camping'); '+ to-V1' (offer, promise, refuse); '+ obj + to-V1' (advise, warn, remind).

Contoh: Report: 'Let's take the scenic cable car,' said Maya, and 'Don't forget your passport!' Dad said to me.

Penyelesaian: Maya suggested taking the scenic cable car. Dad reminded me not to forget my passport.

Tip Penting: NEVER write 'suggested me to go' or 'asked where was I going'!

SUBTOPIK 04

5.4 Strong Adjectives & Travel Account Emails

Good travel writing replaces repetitive 'very' phrases with vivid extreme adjectives and answers every prompt bullet clearly.

  • Strong Adjective Upgrades: very cold -> freezing; very hot -> scorching / boiling; very tired -> exhausted; very scared -> terrified; very funny -> hilarious; very good -> spectacular / unforgettable.
  • Intensifier Rule: Gradable adjectives take 'very / quite' ('very tired'). Extreme adjectives already mean 'extremely', so pair them with 'absolutely / completely / really' ('absolutely freezing', NEVER 'very freezing'!).
  • Informal Email Craft: use lively openings ('Guess what! You won't believe...'), cover every prompt note (destination, highlights, packing tips), and sign off warmly ('Drop me a line soon').

Contoh: Fix the intensifier and upgrade the weak adjective: 'Our mountain hike was very freezing and we felt very exhausted.'

Penyelesaian: Our mountain hike was absolutely freezing and we felt completely exhausted.

Tip Penting: Use 'absolutely / completely' — NEVER 'very' — before strong adjectives like 'freezing', 'exhausted', and 'terrified'!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #1

ELAKKAN: Keeping inverted question order in reported questions ('She asked me where was I staying yesterday').

TEPAT: Use normal statement word order (Subject before Verb) and shift time words: 'She asked me where I had been staying the day before.'

Tip Skor Exam: Reported questions = NO 'do/does/did', Subject + Verb order, and end with a full stop!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #2

ELAKKAN: Writing 'suggest + object + to-infinitive' ('He suggested us to visit Melaka').

TEPAT: 'Suggest' takes '-ing' or 'that + subject + verb': 'He suggested visiting Melaka' or 'He suggested that we visit Melaka.'

Tip Skor Exam: Never put 'to V1' after 'suggest' or 'deny'!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #3

ELAKKAN: Using 'very' before extreme adjectives ('very exhausted', 'very freezing') or mixing 'said' with a person object ('He said me').

TEPAT: Use 'absolutely/completely exhausted', and write 'He told me' (or 'He said to me').

Tip Skor Exam: 'said + (that)' vs 'told + person'; 'very + normal adj' vs 'absolutely + extreme adj'!

Soalan 1 / 20 +10 XP

Read: 'By the time we reached Gate 15, our flight to Kuching had already departed.' Which event happened FIRST?

Huraian: Past Perfect ('had already departed') always marks the earlier past event before the Past Simple action ('reached').

Soalan 2 / 20 +10 XP

Complete accurately: 'It ______ heavily for three days before the mountain trail finally reopened.'

Huraian: Ongoing duration ('for three days') leading up to a past event ('reopened') uses Past Perfect Continuous ('had been snowing').

Soalan 3 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the correct reporting verb: 'Amira ______ me that she had booked two train tickets for Ipoh.'

Huraian: 'Tell' takes an indirect person object ('told me'), whereas 'say' does not take a person directly without 'to'.

Soalan 4 / 20 +10 XP

Report accurately: Sally said, 'I am flying to Langkawi tomorrow.'

Huraian: 'am flying' backshifts to 'was flying', 'tomorrow' becomes 'the following day', and 'said' takes no direct person object.

Soalan 5 / 20 +10 XP

Convert into Reported Speech: 'Where did you buy that backpack?' Tariq asked me.

Huraian: Reported Wh-questions drop 'did', place Subject before Verb ('I had bought'), and end with a full stop.

Soalan 6 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the Yes/No reported question: 'During our Pompeii tour, the guide asked ______ anyone knew about Mount Vesuvius.'

Huraian: Yes/No questions convert using 'if' or 'whether' (never 'that' or 'did').

Soalan 7 / 20 +10 XP

Report the negative warning: 'Don't leave the marked jungle path!' the ranger said to us.

Huraian: Negative reported commands use 'warned/told + object + not + to-infinitive' ('warned us not to leave').

Soalan 8 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence uses the reporting verb 'suggest' CORRECTLY?

Huraian: 'Suggest' is followed directly by a gerund ('suggest + V-ing') or 'that + subject + base verb', never 'object + to-infinitive'.

Soalan 9 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the apology accurately: 'Farah apologised ______ forgetting to pack the travel adapters.'

Huraian: The fixed reporting structure is 'apologise FOR + V-ing / noun'.

Soalan 10 / 20 +10 XP

Which reporting sentence preserves the tense because it states a general truth? 'Our geography teacher told us that ______.'

Huraian: Permanent facts and general scientific truths do not require tense backshift in Reported Speech.

Soalan 11 / 20 +10 XP

Complete with the accurate travel collocation: 'You will be met on ______ at Penang International Airport by our tour guide.' [ARRIVE]

Huraian: 'On arrival' is the standard Module 5 fixed prepositional phrase meaning 'as soon as you arrive'.

Soalan 12 / 20 +10 XP

What does the idiom 'not a piece of cake' mean in: 'Hiking through Pompeii under the midday sun was definitely not a piece of cake'?

Huraian: 'A piece of cake' means very easy; 'not a piece of cake' means difficult or demanding.

Soalan 13 / 20 +10 XP

Which strong adjective accurately replaces 'very hot' when describing desert weather?

Huraian: 'Scorching' (or 'boiling') is the strong adjective equivalent of 'very hot'.

Soalan 14 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the sentence that uses an extreme adjective and modifier CORRECTLY.

Huraian: Extreme adjectives like 'exhausted' pair with 'absolutely / completely' (not 'very'); gradable adjectives like 'tired' take 'very / extremely'.

Soalan 15 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the best travel verb: 'Our coach ______ at 5:30 a.m. sharp so we could catch the sunrise ferry to Pulau Redang.'

Huraian: 'Set off' means to start a journey.

Soalan 16 / 20 +10 XP

Report accurately: 'I must renew my passport before Friday,' said Nadia.

Huraian: Obligation 'must' backshifts to 'had to' in Reported Speech.

Soalan 17 / 20 +10 XP

Which modal remains UNCHANGED when converting from Direct Speech to Reported Speech?

Huraian: 'Should', 'would', 'could', and 'might' do not backshift further in Reported Speech, whereas 'will -> would', 'can -> could', and 'may -> might'.

Soalan 18 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the accurate time shift for Direct Speech 'two days ago' in Reported Speech.

Huraian: 'Ago' shifts back to 'before' or 'earlier' in Reported Speech.

Soalan 19 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the narrative sequence: 'My father, who ______ Melaka years earlier, told us that Jonker Street ______ unforgettable.'

Huraian: Visiting years earlier before speaking uses Past Perfect ('had visited'), and future 'will be' backshifts to 'would be' after 'told us'.

Soalan 20 / 20 +10 XP

When replying to an SPM Paper 2 Part 1 email asking where you travelled, what you enjoyed, and what to pack, what earns full Communicative Achievement?

Huraian: Part 1 email responses require addressing every bullet point in the prompt using an engaging, friendly informal tone.
