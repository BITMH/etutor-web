OBJEKTIF PEMBELAJARAN DSKP

01 Select accurately among full 'to-infinitive', bare infinitive ('let/make + obj + V1') and '-ing' gerund (after prepositions, 'worth', 'avoid') including dual-meaning verbs.
02 Form accurate exclamatory sentences with 'How + adj/adv' and 'What (+ a/an) + adj + noun', avoiding articles before uncountable nature nouns ('weather', 'scenery', 'wildlife').
03 Apply past modals ('modal + have + V3') for deduction ('must/can't have'), narrow escapes ('could have'), regret/criticism ('shouldn't have') and unnecessary completed actions ('needn't have').

SUBTOPIK 01

7.1 Full Infinitive, Bare Infinitive, Gerund & Dual-Meaning Verbs

Paper 1 Editing and Rational Cloze items frequently test what verb form follows specific verbs, prepositions, and meaning-shifting expressions.

  • Full 'to-infinitive': purpose ('called to warn'), after decide/manage/afford/persuade, after too/enough, and after Wh-words (except 'why').
  • Bare Infinitive (no 'to'): modals, 'let / make + object + V1', 'would rather / had better'. ('Help' accepts both!).
  • '-ing' Gerund: sentence subjects, after ALL prepositions ('for cutting', 'no point in complaining', 'interested in recycling'), and after avoid/suggest/risk/worth/can't stand. Dual-meaning pairs: 'stop + -ing' (quit) vs '+ to-V1' (pause to do); 'forget/remember + -ing' (past memory) vs '+ to-V1' (future duty); 'regret + to inform' (formal bad news) vs '+ -ing' (feel sorry for past act).

Contoh: Choose the right forms: 'There is no point in ______ (complain) unless we stop ______ (admire) the view and remember ______ (extinguish) the campfire.'

Penyelesaian: 'complaining' (preposition 'in' + gerund), 'to admire' (pause hiking in order to admire), and 'to extinguish' (remember a duty beforehand).

Tip Penting: Always check 'preposition + V-ing' and test whether 'stop/remember/forget' marks a past memory (-ing) or a duty/purpose (to-V1)!

SUBTOPIK 02

7.2 Exclamatory Sentences & Collective Animal Nouns

Describing Malaysia's rainforests and wildlife calls for accurate exclamations and exact collective nouns.

  • 'How + adjective/adverb (+ S + V)!': 'How gracefully the hornbill glides!' (NO noun inside!).
  • 'What (+ a/an) + adjective + NOUN!': 'What a breathtaking waterfall!' (singular countable) vs 'What stunning coral reefs!' (plural) vs 'What awful weather / spectacular scenery!' (UNCOUNTABLE — NEVER use 'a/an' before 'weather', 'scenery', 'wildlife' or 'pollution'!).
  • Animal Collective Nouns: flock of birds/sheep, herd of elephants/cattle, pack of wolves, swarm of bees/insects, school of fish.

Contoh: Fix the exclamation and noun: 'What an amazing scenery! Look at that flock of elephants crossing the river!'

Penyelesaian: What amazing scenery! Look at that herd of elephants crossing the river!

Tip Penting: 'Scenery/weather/wildlife' = uncountable (NO 'a/an'); elephants form a 'herd', bees a 'swarm', fish a 'school'!

SUBTOPIK 03

7.3 Modals + Have + Past Participle (Past Speculation & Regret)

Commenting on environmental incidents, rescues, or past mistakes relies on past modal structures ending in 'have + V3'.

  • Past Logical Deduction: 'must have + V3' (sure it happened) vs 'can't / couldn't have + V3' (sure it was impossible — NEVER 'mustn't have'!).
  • Possibility & Narrow Escape: 'may / might have + V3' (uncertain); 'could have + V3' (was possible, luckily avoided: 'You could have slipped on that wet moss!').
  • Criticism & Unnecessary Effort: 'should (not) have + V3' ('Factories shouldn't have dumped waste there'). 'needn't have + V3' = it was NOT necessary, YET someone did it anyway ('Maya needn't have watered the lawn — it poured ten minutes later!').

Contoh: Complete: 'You ______ (can't / mustn't) have seen Sam in Sabah yesterday; he's in Tokyo! And we ______ (needn't / shouldn't) have brought umbrellas because the sky stayed dry all day.'

Penyelesaian: 'can't' (past impossibility) and 'needn't' (action done unnecessarily).

Tip Penting: NEVER write 'must of' or 'should of' — always use 'have' ('must have', 'should have')!

SUBTOPIK 04

7.4 Descriptive Eco-Articles & Letters to the Editor

Module 7 Writing trains students to open descriptive articles with sensory hooks and structure formal Letters to the Editor with realistic solutions.

  • Descriptive Article Hooks: rhetorical question ('Have you ever wondered what a 130-million-year-old rainforest sounds like?'), direct address ('Imagine waking up to...'), or exclamation.
  • Rainforest Facts (p. 154): Amazon canopy trees reach ~70 m competing for sunlight; Taiga holds roughly one-third of Earth's trees; 100 tree rings equal one century.
  • Letter to the Editor: open formally ('Dear Editor, I am writing to express my deep concern regarding...'), pair EVERY problem with a practical solution (fines, sorting bins, community cleanups), and sign off 'Yours faithfully,'.

Contoh: Write a formal opening sentence for a Letter to the Editor about plastic waste in local rivers.

Penyelesaian: 'Dear Editor, I am writing to express my deep concern regarding the alarming level of plastic waste polluting our local rivers.'

Tip Penting: Always match every environmental criticism in a Letter to the Editor with at least one actionable solution!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #1

ELAKKAN: Putting 'a/an' before uncountable nature nouns in exclamations ('What an awful weather!' / 'What a spectacular scenery!').

TEPAT: Drop 'a/an' before uncountable nouns: 'What awful weather!' and 'What spectacular scenery!' (or use 'How + adj + it is!').

Tip Skor Exam: 'Weather', 'scenery', 'wildlife' and 'pollution' are uncountable — ban 'a/an'!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #2

ELAKKAN: Using a base verb after prepositions ('That tool is for cut branches' / 'There is no point in complain').

TEPAT: Every preposition takes a '-ing' gerund: 'for cutting branches' and 'no point in complaining'.

Tip Skor Exam: Preposition (in/on/at/for/of/about/without) + V-ING!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #3

ELAKKAN: Writing 'must of / should of' or using 'mustn't have' for past impossibility.

TEPAT: Write 'must have / should have + V3', and express past impossibility with 'can't / couldn't have + V3'.

Tip Skor Exam: Spell 'have' (never 'of') and use 'can't have' for 'impossible in the past'!

Soalan 1 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the accurate verb form: 'There is no point in ______ about river pollution unless our council manages ______ strict patrols.'

Huraian: Preposition 'in' takes a gerund ('complaining'), while 'manage' takes a full 'to-infinitive' ('to organise').

Soalan 2 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the formal notice: 'The national park authority regrets ______ visitors that the waterfall trail is closed today due to fallen trees.'

Huraian: 'Regret + to-infinitive' introduces bad news formally ('regrets to inform'), whereas 'regret + -ing' refers to wishing you hadn't done a past act.

Soalan 3 / 20 +10 XP

Contrast the meaning: 'While trekking up Mount Ledang, we stopped ______ a wild hornbill perched in the canopy.'

Huraian: 'Stop + to-infinitive' means pausing one activity (trekking) in order to do another (take a photo).

Soalan 4 / 20 +10 XP

Complete accurately: 'Please don't forget ______ all campfire embers thoroughly before leaving the campsite.'

Huraian: 'Forget + to-infinitive' refers to a duty or task you must remember to do beforehand.

Soalan 5 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence uses 'let' or 'make' with the accurate infinitive form?

Huraian: Active 'let / make + object' takes the bare infinitive without 'to' ('let tourists feed', 'made everyone pack').

Soalan 6 / 20 +10 XP

Spot the accurate exclamation at sunrise on Mount Kinabalu.

Huraian: 'Scenery' and 'weather' are uncountable nouns, so they never take 'a/an' in 'What + adj + noun!'.

Soalan 7 / 20 +10 XP

Order accurately: '______ the white-bellied sea eagle glides above the mangrove bay!'

Huraian: 'How' modifies an adverb ('gracefully') directly before Subject + Verb ('the sea eagle glides').

Soalan 8 / 20 +10 XP

Match the accurate collective noun: 'A colourful ______ of parrotfish swam silently over the coral reef.'

Huraian: Fish gather in a 'school' (or shoal); elephants in a 'herd', wolves in a 'pack', and bees in a 'swarm'.

Soalan 9 / 20 +10 XP

Identify the ONLY sentence with both accurate exclamation grammar and collective wildlife vocabulary.

Huraian: 'Sight' is singular countable ('What an unforgettable sight') and 'swarm of bees' is exact; 'pollution' is uncountable, elephants form a 'herd', and 'How' does not take 'a'.

Soalan 10 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the right past deduction: 'You ______ spotted Farid at Taman Negara yesterday; he flew to Kuching two days ago!'

Huraian: Past logical impossibility uses 'can't have + V3' or 'couldn't have + V3' (never 'mustn't have').

Soalan 11 / 20 +10 XP

Complete cleanly: 'Aina ______ watered the garden this afternoon; look at the heavy rain soaking the soil now!'

Huraian: 'Needn't have + V3' expresses that an action WAS done in the past even though it turned out to be unnecessary.

Soalan 12 / 20 +10 XP

Which past modal expresses regret or criticism about an environmental mistake?

Huraian: 'Shouldn't have + V3' criticises or regrets a harmful past action.

Soalan 13 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence highlights a narrow escape that thankfully did NOT happen?

Huraian: 'Could have + V3' expresses a past possibility or danger that was narrowly avoided.

Soalan 14 / 20 +10 XP

Which preposition completes this Module 7 fixed expression: 'Endangered Malayan tigers must be protected ______ all costs.'?

Huraian: The fixed prepositional phrase is 'at all costs'.

Soalan 15 / 20 +10 XP

According to the Module 7 Trees Video Worksheet, how tall do Amazon canopy trees grow while competing for sunlight?

Huraian: Module 7 p. 154 states Amazon canopy trees reach roughly 70 m fighting for sunlight, and 100 tree rings mark one century.

Soalan 16 / 20 +10 XP

Fix the preposition + verb error: 'These sturdy bamboo poles are ideal for ______ eco-friendly garden trellises.'

Huraian: After the preposition 'for', use the '-ing' gerund ('for building').

Soalan 17 / 20 +10 XP

Form an accurate adjective from [POISON]: 'Never touch brightly coloured forest frogs unless a guide confirms they are not ______.'

Huraian: Module 7 teaches adjective suffixes including '-ous' ('poisonous').

Soalan 18 / 20 +10 XP

Which opening line hooks readers BEST for a descriptive article about Malaysia's ancient rainforests?

Huraian: Descriptive articles engage readers immediately with a vivid rhetorical question ('Have you ever imagined + -ing...?') and sensory imagery.

Soalan 19 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence fits a formal Letter to the Editor demanding cleaner neighbourhood parks?

Huraian: A Letter to the Editor uses formal register and pairs each concern with constructive, realistic solutions.

Soalan 20 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the sentence with ZERO verb-pattern errors: 'Our eco-club ______ to clean the mangrove coast, and everyone enjoyed ______ new saplings.'

Huraian: 'Manage' takes a full 'to-infinitive' ('managed to clean'), while 'enjoy' takes a gerund ('enjoyed planting').
