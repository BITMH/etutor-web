OBJEKTIF PEMBELAJARAN DSKP

01 Contrast Present Perfect Simple (completed achievements, counts, stative verbs) with Present Perfect Continuous (ongoing training duration and visible side-effects like sweat/mud).
02 Switch accurately between Present Perfect ('breaking news') and Past Simple ('When did he join? / last month / in 2012') in sports reporting.
03 Apply Unit 3 sports vocabulary ('fit' vs 'suit', 'athletics track', 'pitch', 'court', 'rink'), word families ('indication', 'athletic', 'sponsorship') and phrasal verbs ('stand in for', 'pass up', 'put off', 'take to').
04 Write formal opinion essays on sports ethics and media privacy using prompt paraphrasing, PEEL paragraphs and C1-style expressions ('be entitled to').
SUBTOPIK 01

3.1 Present Perfect Simple vs. Present Perfect Continuous

Like a sports commentator, Unit 3 separates trophy totals and instant injuries (Simple) from training hours and visible exhaustion (Continuous).

  • Present Perfect Simple ('has/have + V3'): counts & completed results ('Lee Chong Wei has won this title twelve times'; 'I have left my racket at home' — stative/momentary!). Key markers: 'just, already, yet, ever, never, still'. Note position: 'The race STILL hasn't started' ('still' before 'hasn't' vs 'yet' at the end).
  • Present Perfect Continuous ('has/have been + V-ing'): stresses duration ('Our squad has been training for four hours') or visible present consequences ('Your shoes are muddy — you've been playing football!').
  • 'has been' vs 'has gone': 'Sally has been to the gym' (visited and back now) vs 'Sally has gone to the gym' (still there).

Contoh: Choose the right tense: 'Tariq ______ (train) as reserve goalkeeper all season, and he ______ (win) three clean-sheet awards so far.'

Penyelesaian: 'has been training' (ongoing season-long effort) and 'has won' (completed tally 'three ... so far').

Tip Penting: Count/Result/Stative = Simple; Duration/Sweat/Mud = Continuous; 'still' goes BEFORE 'hasn't'!

SUBTOPIK 02

3.2 Past Simple vs. Present Perfect (Match Interviews)

Unit 3 highlights the exact boundary between ongoing present relevance and finished past timestamps during sports interviews.

  • News-to-Detail Shift (p. 52): Announce breaking news in Present Perfect ('Jason has joined the hockey team!'), then switch immediately to Past Simple when asking 'When...?' or stating exact past times ('When DID he join?' -> 'He JOINED last week').
  • Finished Time Markers: 'last month', 'yesterday', 'in 2012', 'two years ago' ALWAYS demand Past Simple ('Although the captain hurt his ankle last month, he has recovered now').
  • 'Since' Clauses: 'We haven't played them since 2012, when we won 3-0' — Present Perfect main clause + Past Simple inside the 'since/when' past event.

Contoh: Complete the post-match dialogue: 'Reporter: Coach, how long ______ (know) the new striker? Coach: Since 2021, when he ______ (score) twice in the state final.'

Penyelesaian: 'have you known' (stative Present Perfect up to now) and 'scored' (finished past time 'in 2021').

Tip Penting: The moment a sentence asks 'When...?' or names a finished year/month, lock in Past Simple!

SUBTOPIK 03

3.3 Sportswear Lexis, Venues & Sports Phrasal Verbs

Unit 3 Vocabulary builds accuracy across venues, equipment fit vs style, word families, and multi-word verbs.

  • Venues & Kit: athletics track, football pitch, tennis court, ice-skating rink; 'fit' = physical size ('too tight, need a bigger size') vs 'suit' = looks good/flattering; 'compatible WITH' (earphones working with a smartwatch); 'be in debt' (owe money).
  • Phrasal Verbs (p. 51): 'stand in for' (substitute an injured player), 'take to' (begin to like), 'pass up' (miss a chance), 'stand out' (be noticeable), 'stand for' (represent, e.g. F.C.), 'put someone off (+ -ing)' (discourage).
  • Word Building (p. 51): indicate -> indication (not 'indicator' for abstract sign!), athlete -> athletic/athletics, sponsor -> sponsorship, contribute -> contribution.

Contoh: Fix the confusable words: 'These new boots don't suit my feet — they are too narrow — and the high fee put me out joining the club.'

Penyelesaian: These new boots don't fit my feet — they are too narrow — and the high fee put me off joining the club.

Tip Penting: 'Fit' = size; 'Suit' = style; 'put off + -ing' = discourage; 'stand in for' = substitute!

SUBTOPIK 04

3.4 Opinion Essays: Sports Stars & Media Privacy

Unit 3 Writing trains candidates to paraphrase essay prompts, develop PEEL body paragraphs, and sustain formal academic register.

  • Introduction: Paraphrase the prompt formally instead of copying ('The media tells readers how much money footballers earn' -> 'News outlets regularly disclose athletes' substantial salaries') + clear thesis ('I firmly believe sportspeople are entitled to personal privacy').
  • PEEL Body Paragraphs: Point (Topic sentence) -> Explanation -> Example -> Link back to thesis.
  • Register Upgrade: Replace informal 'lots of money' or 'super unfair' with 'substantial earnings' and 'an unjustified intrusion', and summarise without brand-new topics in the conclusion.

Contoh: Upgrade this weak thesis line: 'In my view, football stars get lots of money, so newspapers can print everything about their families.'

Penyelesaian: 'While elite athletes earn substantial salaries from public sponsorship, I firmly believe they remain entitled to personal privacy off the pitch.'

Tip Penting: Use formal structures like 'be entitled TO + noun' and 'substantial salaries' in SPM Part 3 essays!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #1

ELAKKAN: Using Present Perfect with a finished past timestamp ('The striker has broken his wrist last month').

TEPAT: Finished past time markers ('last month', 'in 2012', 'yesterday') require Past Simple: 'The striker broke his wrist last month.'

Tip Skor Exam: Specific finished time = Past Simple; Present result with no timestamp = Present Perfect!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #2

ELAKKAN: Misplacing 'still' after 'hasn't' ('The match hasn't still started') or confusing 'been' with 'gone'.

TEPAT: Place 'still' BEFORE the negative auxiliary ('The match still hasn't started'), and use 'has been' only if the person has returned.

Tip Skor Exam: 'still hasn't' vs 'hasn't ... yet'; 'been' = returned, 'gone' = still there!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #3

ELAKKAN: Confusing 'fit' with 'suit' or writing 'put me out' instead of 'put me off + -ing'.

TEPAT: Use 'fit' for size/comfort, 'suit' for appearance/style, and 'put someone off + -ing' for discouraging someone.

Tip Skor Exam: Size = fit; Style = suit; Discourage = put off + V-ing!

Soalan 1 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the accurate tense for a trophy count: 'Our national squash legend Nicol David ______ the World Open title eight times.'

Huraian: Focusing on a completed achievement count ('eight times') up to a career milestone uses Present Perfect Simple ('has won') or Past Simple for retired career — here Present Perfect Simple ('has won') directly contrasts count vs duration.

Soalan 2 / 20 +10 XP

Select the sentence highlighting a visible physical consequence of recent ongoing training.

Huraian: Visible present effects of recent continuous effort (sweat, mud, breathlessness) use Present Perfect Continuous ('Have you been sprinting?').

Soalan 3 / 20 +10 XP

Check word order: Which negative Present Perfect sentence uses 'still' ACCURATELY?

Huraian: In negative Present Perfect sentences, 'still' sits immediately BEFORE 'hasn't / haven't' ('still hasn't started').

Soalan 4 / 20 +10 XP

Complete cleanly: 'Sally ______ to the sports hall, and now she is stretching calmly in her living room.'

Huraian: Because Sally has already returned home, use 'has been' (visited and came back).

Soalan 5 / 20 +10 XP

Which verb completes this stative Present Perfect sentence? 'Oh no! I ______ my badminton racket inside the locker room!'

Huraian: Momentary/stative result actions ('leave something behind') use Present Perfect Simple ('have left'), never Continuous.

Soalan 6 / 20 +10 XP

Spot the breaking-news-to-detail transition: A: 'Jason ______ the state hockey squad!' B: 'Really? When ______ he join?'

Huraian: Announce news with Present Perfect ('has joined'), then switch to Past Simple for 'When did he join?'.

Soalan 7 / 20 +10 XP

Complete accurately: 'Although the captain ______ his ankle last month, he ______ fully now and will start the final.'

Huraian: 'Last month' demands Past Simple ('hurt'), while 'now' shows current recovery result ('has recovered').

Soalan 8 / 20 +10 XP

Match the venue correctly: Where do ice hockey and figure-skating competitions take place?

Huraian: Ice sports happen on a 'rink'; football/rugby on a 'pitch'; tennis/badminton on a 'court'; running on an 'athletics track'.

Soalan 9 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the right confusable verb: 'These football boots don't ______ me — the toes feel far too tight, so I need a size larger.'

Huraian: 'Fit' refers to physical size and measurements; 'suit' refers to style, colour, or appearance.

Soalan 10 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the phrasal verb: 'Because our regular goalkeeper sprained his wrist, Tariq had to ______ him during the semi-final.'

Huraian: 'Stand in for' means to substitute or temporarily replace an absent teammate.

Soalan 11 / 20 +10 XP

What does 'pass up' mean in: 'Farah refused to pass up the chance to train with the national squad'?

Huraian: 'Pass up' (an opportunity/offer) means to let a good chance go by or decline it.

Soalan 12 / 20 +10 XP

Complete accurately: 'The steep monthly membership fee ______ joining the private fitness club.'

Huraian: 'Put someone off (+ -ing)' means to discourage them from doing something.

Soalan 13 / 20 +10 XP

Form the accurate noun from [INDICATE]: 'The sold-out stadium is a clear ______ of how passionately Malaysians support badminton.'

Huraian: 'A clear indication of' uses the abstract noun 'indication' (meaning sign/evidence), whereas 'indicator' is a physical gauge or car blinker.

Soalan 14 / 20 +10 XP

Which preposition completes the adjective pairing: 'Are these wireless earbuds compatible ______ my running smartwatch?'

Huraian: 'Compatible' pairs with the preposition 'with' ('compatible with').

Soalan 15 / 20 +10 XP

Rewrite without changing meaning: 'After losing his sponsorship deal, the cyclist owed a great deal of money.' -> 'The cyclist was ______.'

Huraian: Unit 3 Review (p. 50–51) pairs 'owe money' with 'be in debt'.

Soalan 16 / 20 +10 XP

Complete the 'since' clause structure: 'Our school hasn't beaten SMK Jaya in hockey since 2018, when we ______ 3-1 in the district cup.'

Huraian: After 'since 2018, when...', the past reference point uses Past Simple ('won').

Soalan 17 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the sentence that paraphrases 'The media tells readers how much money footballers earn' with the best SPM formal register.

Huraian: 'News outlets frequently disclose athletes' substantial salaries' replaces both nouns and verbs cleanly in formal essay register.

Soalan 18 / 20 +10 XP

Complete this formal Unit 3 thesis statement: 'Professional athletes are fully ______ personal privacy away from the stadium.'

Huraian: The formal C1-style expression is 'be entitled TO + noun/gerund'.

Soalan 19 / 20 +10 XP

Which PEEL essay paragraph flaw weakens an opinion essay on sports ethics?

Huraian: Conclusions should synthesise previously argued points rather than introducing brand-new arguments.

Soalan 20 / 20 +10 XP

Spot the ONLY sentence that is 100% accurate across Unit 3 tenses and vocabulary.

Huraian: Option D contrasts ongoing effort ('has been training ... all month') with completed goal count ('has already scored four goals').
