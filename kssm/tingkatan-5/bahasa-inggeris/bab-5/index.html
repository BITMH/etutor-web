SUBTOPIK 01

5.1 Environmental Threats, Conservation & Eco-Lexis

Unit 5 equips SPM candidates with exact terminology to discuss climate risks, urban planning, and renewable solutions.

  • Environmental Threats: prolonged 'drought' ruins crops and increases 'famine' risk; unchecked urban sprawl threatens protected 'green belts' surrounding cities.
  • Clean Solutions: 'wind farms' generate renewable power; 'park rangers' patrol forest reserves against illegal logging; households 'sort out' recyclable plastics and glass.
  • Register Upgrade: replace vague 'bad for nature' with 'habitat loss', 'renewable energy', and 'conservation'.

Contoh: Name the Unit 5 role and land zone: (1) Staff hired to protect forest reserves and monitor endangered hornbills. (2) Protected countryside surrounding a city where building is restricted.

Penyelesaian: (1) Park rangers; (2) Green belt.

Tip Penting: Precise eco-lexis lifts both Paper 1 Reading and Paper 2 essay Lexical Resource marks!

SUBTOPIK 02

5.2 Future Simple ('will'), 'Be Going To' & 'Shall'

Choosing among 'will', 'be going to', and 'shall' depends on spontaneity, prior planning, visible clues, and first-person offers.

  • 'will + V1': spontaneous decisions/offers made right now ('Those recycling crates look heavy — I'll help you carry them!'), promises, and personal opinions ('I expect solar costs will fall').
  • 'be going to + V1': prior plans ('We are going to plant mangroves this Saturday') and predictions backed by present visible proof ('Look at black smoke pouring from that chimney — it's going to pollute the valley!').
  • 'Shall I / Shall we + V1': polite offers ('Shall I sort the bottles?') and group suggestions ('Shall we organise a beach cleanup?'). Never use 'Shall' with 'he/she/they'!

Contoh: Choose 'will', 'going to', or 'shall': (1) '______ we start an eco-club composting bin?' (2) 'Look at those storm clouds — it ______ rain!' (3) 'Wait, I ______ grab an umbrella for you.'

Penyelesaian: (1) 'Shall' (group suggestion with 'we'); (2) 'is going to' (visible clouds); (3) ''ll / will' (spontaneous offer).

Tip Penting: I/We suggestion = Shall; Visible proof/Plan = be going to; On-the-spot offer = will!

SUBTOPIK 03

5.3 Future Continuous, Future Perfect & Future Perfect Continuous

Unit 5 contrasts actions in progress AT a future point against totals finished BY a deadline and durations measured up to a future date.

  • Future Continuous ('will be + V-ing'): action in progress AT a specific future moment ('This time tomorrow, we will be planting saplings along the estuary').
  • Future Perfect Simple ('will have + V3'): action completed BEFORE a stated deadline ('By December, the town will have planted 12,000 trees').
  • Future Perfect Continuous ('will have been + V-ing'): ongoing duration up to a future point ('By 2035, coastal technicians will have been maintaining the sea wall for twelve years').

Contoh: Spot 'at' vs 'by': (1) 'At 9 a.m. tomorrow, rangers ______ (patrol) the reserve.' (2) 'By 2030, the state ______ (build) three wind farms.'

Penyelesaian: (1) 'will be patrolling' (mid-action at 9 a.m.); (2) 'will have built' (completed before 2030).

Tip Penting: 'This time tomorrow / at 3 p.m.' = will be + V-ing; 'By + deadline' = will have + V3!

SUBTOPIK 04

5.4 Temporals & The Golden 'No-Will' Rule

One of the highest-frequency SPM grammar traps involves future time clauses introduced by temporal conjunctions.

  • Core Temporals: 'when, before, after, until, as soon as, once, by the time'.
  • THE GOLDEN RULE: Even when referring to the future, NEVER put 'will' inside the temporal clause! Use Present Simple (or Present Perfect to stress completion first): 'Once the council approves (or has approved) the grant, we will install solar panels.'
  • Main Result Clause: keeps 'will + V1' (or Future Perfect) normally.

Contoh: Fix the temporal error: 'We will distribute reusable flasks as soon as the shipment will arrive.'

Penyelesaian: We will distribute reusable flasks as soon as the shipment arrives. (Drop 'will' after 'as soon as'!)

Tip Penting: Scan immediately after 'when / until / once / as soon as / by the time' — BAN 'will'!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #1

ELAKKAN: Putting 'will' inside a future temporal clause ('Once the mangrove belt will recover, storm damage will drop').

TEPAT: Replace 'will' inside the temporal clause with Present Simple (or Present Perfect): 'Once the mangrove belt recovers, storm damage will drop.'

Tip Skor Exam: Temporal + Present Simple/Perfect, [Main Clause + will]!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #2

ELAKKAN: Confusing Future Continuous ('will be + V-ing') with Future Perfect ('will have + V3') before 'by + deadline'.

TEPAT: Use 'will have + V3' when an action finishes BEFORE 'by tomorrow / by 2030', and 'will be + V-ing' for mid-action snapshots ('this time tomorrow').

Tip Skor Exam: BY deadline = will have + V3; AT future moment = will be + V-ing!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #3

ELAKKAN: Using 'will' for polite 'we' suggestions ('Will we plant trees this Sunday?') or for predictions with obvious visible proof.

TEPAT: Use 'Shall we...?' for polite first-person suggestions and 'be going to' when present visual evidence proves the outcome.

Tip Skor Exam: Suggestion with 'I/we'? Use 'Shall'!
