OBJEKTIF PEMBELAJARAN DSKP

01 Differentiate Unit 7 media roles ('correspondent', 'columnist', 'editor', 'newsreader', 'presenter'), broadcast nouns ('commercials', 'soap operas', 'reviews') and trend prepositions ('decrease in', 'rise in').
02 Form Passive Voice accurately across tenses, passive gerunds ('dislikes being interrupted'), passive infinitives ('needs to be proofread'), 'get'-passives and distinguish 'by + agent' from 'with + instrument'.
03 Convert reporting sentences smoothly between Impersonal Passive ('It is believed that...') and Personal Passive ('Podcasts are believed to be growing' / 'to have lost' for past events), avoiding 'that' inside Personal Passives.
04 Apply fact-checking principles and write balanced documentary or media reviews with spoiler-free summaries and justified audience verdicts.
SUBTOPIK 01

7.1 Media Professions, Broadcast Genres & Prepositions

Unit 7 separates print, radio, television, and digital media roles alongside fixed prepositions frequently tested in Paper 1.

  • Media Roles: 'correspondent' (reports live from overseas or specialist locations), 'columnist' (writes recurring analytical opinion pieces), 'editor' (selects and refines articles before publication), 'newsreader' (reads studio news bulletins aloud), 'presenter' (hosts live studio shows or documentaries).
  • Media Nouns: 'commercials' (paid broadcast adverts), 'reviews' (critical evaluations), 'soap operas' (ongoing dramatic TV serials).
  • Trend Prepositions: nouns showing change take 'IN' before the measured category — 'a sharp decrease IN print readership', 'a rise IN podcast subscriptions' (NEVER 'decrease of' here!).

Contoh: Identify the error: 'Our overseas newsreader sent a live report from Tokyo about the drop of newspaper sales.'

Penyelesaian: Our overseas correspondent sent a live report from Tokyo about the drop in newspaper sales.

Tip Penting: Field reporter = correspondent; Studio bulletin reader = newsreader; drop/decrease/rise IN!

SUBTOPIK 02

7.2 Passive Voice Across Tenses, Get-Passive & 'By' vs 'With'

Unit 7 extends passive mastery beyond simple tenses into gerunds, infinitives, accidental 'get'-passives, and agent-vs-tool prepositions.

  • Passive Gerunds & Infinitives: after gerund verbs ('The editor avoids being misquoted' = 'being + V3'); after infinitive verbs ('The script needs to be proofread' = 'to be + V3').
  • 'Get'-Passive ('get + V3'): highlights sudden or accidental events ('His camera lens got smashed during the storm'). Note: Future Continuous, Present Perfect Continuous, and Past Perfect Continuous have NO standard passive!
  • Agent vs Instrument: use 'BY' for the human doer/cause ('filmed by a drone operator'), and 'WITH' for the physical tool or material ('edited with open-source software', 'repaired with a copper cable').

Contoh: Complete: 'The live satellite dish was repaired quickly ______ a spare cable, and the journalist dislikes ______ (interrupt) on air.'

Penyelesaian: 'with' (physical tool/instrument) and 'being interrupted' (passive gerund after 'dislike').

Tip Penting: Person/Doer = BY; Tool/Material = WITH; Avoid/Dislike + BEING + V3!

SUBTOPIK 03

7.3 Impersonal & Personal Passive Reporting Structures

Journalists and essay writers report public opinion objectively without naming one source using Impersonal and Personal Passives.

  • Impersonal Passive: 'It + passive reporting verb + THAT-clause' ('It is thought that online ads influence young buyers').
  • Personal Passive (Present/General): 'Subject + passive verb + TO + V1' ('Online ads ARE thought to influence young buyers'). Watch plural agreement ('Podcasts ARE said to be growing')!
  • Personal Passive (Earlier Past Action): 'Subject + passive verb + TO HAVE + V3' ('The streamer is believed to have lost one million subscribers last quarter'). IRON RULE: Drop 'that' completely inside Personal Passives!

Contoh: Convert BOTH ways: 'Analysts report that the leaked clip spread overnight.'

Penyelesaian: (1) Impersonal: It is reported that the leaked clip spread overnight. (2) Personal: The leaked clip is reported to have spread overnight.

Tip Penting: Personal Passive = NO 'that' + 'to V1' (general/now) OR 'to have V3' (past event)!

SUBTOPIK 04

7.4 Media Literacy & Critical Documentary Reviews

Unit 7 Writing pairs digital fact-checking with structured, balanced reviews that evaluate craft rather than retelling the whole plot.

  • Media Literacy Checks: verify viral claims against two independent correspondents, inspect publication dates, and spot sponsored commercials disguised as genuine reviews.
  • Review Blueprint: open with brief title/genre context in Present Simple without spoilers, then devote two-thirds of the review to concrete strengths (cinematography, balanced interviews) and one constructive limitation ('Although the soundtrack occasionally overpowers narration...').
  • Recommendation Pattern: end with a target-audience recommendation ('I would strongly recommend this documentary to students interested in...') — NEVER write 'recommend you to watch'!

Contoh: Fix the recommendation verb pattern: 'I strongly recommend teenagers to watch this investigative documentary.'

Penyelesaian: 'I strongly recommend this investigative documentary to teenagers' (or 'recommend watching...').

Tip Penting: Use 'recommend + noun + TO + audience' and dedicate two-thirds of a review to evaluation, not plot!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #1

ELAKKAN: Using 'by' before a physical tool or instrument ('The broadcast was recorded by a smartphone').

TEPAT: Reserve 'by' for the doer/agent and use 'with' for instruments/tools: 'The broadcast was recorded with a smartphone by a field correspondent.'

Tip Skor Exam: Person = BY; Tool/Object = WITH!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #2

ELAKKAN: Blending Impersonal and Personal Passives ('The channel is believed that to lose viewers') or forgetting 'to have V3' for past actions.

TEPAT: Keep 'It is believed that...' separate from 'The channel is believed to have lost viewers last quarter' (zero 'that'!).

Tip Skor Exam: Start with 'It'? Use 'that'. Start with the subject? Drop 'that' and use 'to V1 / to have V3'!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #3

ELAKKAN: Writing 'decrease of newspaper sales' or 'recommend someone to watch'.

TEPAT: Trend nouns take 'IN' ('decrease in newspaper sales'), and 'recommend' takes 'recommend [film] to [audience]' or 'recommend + -ing'.

Tip Skor Exam: Decrease/Rise + IN; Recommend + noun + TO + person!

Huraian: Trend nouns such as 'decrease', 'increase', 'rise', and 'drop' take 'in' before the measured topic.

Soalan 3 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence uses Unit 7 broadcast terminology ACCURATELY?

Huraian: 'Reviews' are critical evaluations; 'commercials' are paid ads, 'soap operas' are continuing dramas, and 'newsreaders' read bulletins.

Soalan 4 / 20 +10 XP

Complete using a passive gerund: 'The investigative journalist strongly dislikes ______ during live interviews.'

Huraian: 'Dislike' takes a gerund ('-ing'); its passive form is 'being + V3' ('being interrupted').

Soalan 5 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the accurate passive infinitive: 'Every draft news bulletin needs ______ before going live at 8 p.m.'

Huraian: After 'needs' (active subject = the bulletin), passive meaning takes 'to be + V3' ('needs to be proofread').

Soalan 6 / 20 +10 XP

Select the accurate preposition for an instrument: 'The broken satellite dish was repaired quickly ______ a spare copper cable.'

Huraian: Physical tools and materials used to carry out an action require 'with' ('with a spare copper cable'), not 'by'.

Soalan 7 / 20 +10 XP

Which tense has NO standard passive voice form in English?

Huraian: Future Continuous ('will be + V-ing') and Perfect Continuous tenses do not have standard passive equivalents.

Soalan 8 / 20 +10 XP

Choose the sentence that uses a 'get'-passive naturally for an unexpected event.

Huraian: 'Get + V3' ('got knocked over') naturally expresses sudden or accidental passive events.

Soalan 9 / 20 +10 XP

Convert into an accurate Personal Passive: 'Analysts believe that the streaming platform lost one million subscribers last quarter.'

Huraian: Personal Passive drops 'that' and uses the perfect infinitive 'to have + V3' ('to have lost') because the loss happened in the past ('last quarter').

Soalan 10 / 20 +10 XP

Check subject-verb agreement in Personal Passive: 'It is said that audio podcasts ______ rapidly among commuters.' -> 'Audio podcasts ______ growing rapidly.'

Huraian: Fronting plural 'Audio podcasts' requires plural 'are said' + 'to be'.

Soalan 11 / 20 +10 XP

Identify the ONLY sentence free of Impersonal/Personal Passive blending errors.

Huraian: Impersonal Passive uses 'It + is reported + that-clause'; Options A and B illegally blend the two patterns.

Soalan 12 / 20 +10 XP

Complete BOTH blanks: 'The live footage ______ with a handheld phone before it ______ to have reached half a million viewers.'

Huraian: Past passive with tool takes 'was captured with', followed by Personal Passive 'was reported to have reached'.

Soalan 13 / 20 +10 XP

A viral social post shows a shocking flood photo with no journalist name, no date, and 'SHARE NOW BEFORE DELETED!' What is the best media-literacy step?

Huraian: Critical media literacy requires verifying anonymous viral claims across independent dated sources before amplifying them.

Soalan 14 / 20 +10 XP

Why should students inspect reviews on blogs for hidden 'commercials' or sponsorships?

Huraian: Distinguishing independent reviews from paid sponsorships prevents misleading buying decisions.

Soalan 15 / 20 +10 XP

Which recommendation sentence follows accurate English grammar for a documentary review?

Huraian: Use 'recommend + noun + to + audience' (or 'recommend + -ing'), never 'recommend someone to V1'.

Soalan 16 / 20 +10 XP

Which sentence shows the strongest evaluative balance inside a documentary review?

Huraian: Pairing a specific minor critique ('Although...') with concrete strengths creates authoritative, balanced evaluation.

Soalan 17 / 20 +10 XP

Convert into Active Voice cleanly: 'The morning headlines are edited by Priya before 6 a.m.'

Huraian: Present Simple Passive ('are edited') converts back to Present Simple Active ('Priya edits').

Soalan 18 / 20 +10 XP

Keep the tense exact when transforming to Passive: 'Technicians are upgrading the radio transmitter right now.'

Huraian: Present Continuous Active ('are upgrading') becomes Present Continuous Passive ('is being upgraded').

Soalan 19 / 20 +10 XP

Complete accurately: 'The podcast host refuses ______ pressured into endorsing unverified health supplements.'

Huraian: 'Refuse' takes a full 'to-infinitive', whose passive form is 'to be + V3' ('refuses to be pressured').

Soalan 20 / 20 +10 XP

Spot the ONLY sentence that is 100% accurate across Unit 7 grammar and vocabulary.

Huraian: Option D accurately pairs 'correspondents' with Personal Passive ('are widely believed to hold'); A swaps 'in/by/with', B blends 'It is said... to have', and C uses 'with' for a person.
