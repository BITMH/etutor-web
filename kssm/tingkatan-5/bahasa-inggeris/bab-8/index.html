SUBTOPIK 01

8.1 Careers, Remuneration & Workplace Collocations

Unit 8 builds precise professional vocabulary for job advertisements, workplace news, and career interviews.

  • 'Salary' vs 'Wages': a 'salary' is fixed monthly or annual pay for salaried staff; 'wages' are hourly, daily, or weekly pay (common in part-time or shift roles).
  • Employment Changes: earn 'qualifications' (certificates/degrees), 'impress the interviewer', 'get promoted' (advance upward), 'get fired' (dismissed for misconduct) vs 'be made redundant' (job cut because the role is no longer needed, not personal fault).
  • Preposition Trap: 'apply FOR' a vacancy/post ('apply for the technician post') vs 'apply TO' a company or university ('apply to Biotech Utara') — NEVER 'apply a job'!

Contoh: Fix both workplace errors: 'After failing to apply the cafe job, Amir got fired when the shop closed down due to low sales.'

Penyelesaian: After failing to apply for the cafe job, Amir was made redundant when the shop closed down due to low sales.

Tip Penting: Apply FOR a post / TO a firm; business downsizing = made redundant, NOT fired; hourly pay = wages!

SUBTOPIK 02

8.2 Conditionals 0–3 & Conditional Connectors

Before mastering Mixed Conditionals, Unit 8 consolidates all four standard conditional types plus formal conditional linkers.

  • Real Conditionals: Type 0 (If + V1 -> V1, company policy/facts) and Type 1 (If + V1 -> will + V1, real likely future).
  • Unreal Conditionals: Type 2 (If + V2/'were' -> would + V1, unreal present) and Type 3 (If + had + V3 -> would have + V3, unreal past).
  • Connectors ('unless' = if not; 'provided / providing (that)' & 'as/so long as' = only if): ALWAYS take Present Simple for future meaning — NEVER put 'will' or double negatives after them ('Unless you prepare...', NEVER 'Unless you don't will prepare').

Contoh: Fix the connector error: 'You will keep the internship provided that you won't miss deadlines, unless illness will prevent you.'

Penyelesaian: You will keep the internship provided that you don't miss deadlines, unless illness prevents you.

Tip Penting: After 'unless', 'provided that', and 'as long as', drop 'will' completely!

SUBTOPIK 03

8.3 Mixed Conditionals: Bridging Past & Present Timeframes

A Mixed Conditional links two DIFFERENT time zones — most commonly an unreal past decision whose consequence is still visible RIGHT NOW.

  • Pattern A (Unreal Past Action -> Present Result NOW): 'If + Past Perfect (had + V3), would + V1 / would be + V-ing (+ now/today)'. Example: 'If Mei Ling had checked the contract last month, she wouldn't be working unpaid overtime right now.'
  • Pattern B (Permanent Present Trait -> Unreal Past Result): 'If + Past Simple (V2/'were'), would have + V3'. Example: 'If Amir weren't so organised, he wouldn't have impressed the interview panel yesterday.'
  • Time-Bridge Check: Always scan the main clause for 'now', 'today', or 'currently' — that single cue flips 'would have + V3' into 'would + V1 / be + V-ing'!

Contoh: Spot the time cue 'today': 'If Amir ______ (complete) his engineering degree earlier, he ______ (earn) a senior salary today.'

Penyelesaian: 'had completed' (unreal past choice) and 'would be earning / would earn' (present result marked by 'today').

Tip Penting: 'If + had + V3' + 'today/now' = Mixed Conditional ('would + V1 / be + V-ing')!

SUBTOPIK 04

8.4 Writing Formal Job Application Letters

Form 5 concludes with SPM's flagship formal cover letter: structured, persuasive, and strictly free of contractions or tone slips.

  • Salutation & Sign-off Rule: 'Dear Sir/Madam,' -> 'Yours faithfully,'; 'Dear Ms Tan,' -> 'Yours sincerely,'.
  • Paragraph Blueprint: (1) State the exact post and where seen ('I am writing to apply for the post of Assistant Designer advertised on...'); (2) Match qualifications & part-time/volunteer skills to the job; (3) Highlight soft skills (teamwork, punctuality); (4) Express interview availability formally ('I would welcome the opportunity to attend an interview') and close.
  • Iron Register Rule: ZERO contractions ('I am', never 'I'm / can't') and ZERO slang.

Contoh: Spot two register errors in: 'Dear Ms Lim, I'm writing to apply a summer intern role! Yours faithfully, Daniel.'

Penyelesaian: Fix 'I'm' -> 'I am', 'apply a' -> 'apply for a', remove '!', and change 'Yours faithfully' -> 'Yours sincerely' (because 'Ms Lim' is named!).

Tip Penting: Named recipient = Yours sincerely; Unnamed = Yours faithfully; zero contractions!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #1

ELAKKAN: Using Type 3 'would have + V3' in a Mixed Conditional even when 'now' or 'today' appears in the result ('If I had taken that course, I would have earned more today').

TEPAT: Because 'today' marks a present outcome, switch the main clause to Type 2: 'If I had taken that course, I would be earning / would earn more today.'

Tip Skor Exam: Past 'If + had V3' + NOW/TODAY = Mixed Conditional ('would + V1 / be + V-ing')!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #2

ELAKKAN: Putting 'will' after 'as long as / provided that / unless' ('I'll hire you as long as you will arrive on time').

TEPAT: Conditional connectors use Present Simple for future time: 'I'll hire you as long as you arrive on time.'

Tip Skor Exam: Ban 'will' after 'unless', 'as long as', and 'provided that'!

AMARAN PEMERIKSA #3

ELAKKAN: Confusing 'made redundant' with 'got fired' or omitting 'for/to' after 'apply'.

TEPAT: 'Made redundant' means the company no longer needs the job role; 'fired' means dismissal for misconduct. Always 'apply for' a role and 'apply to' an employer.

Tip Skor Exam: Downsizing = redundant; Misconduct = fired; apply FOR post / TO firm!
