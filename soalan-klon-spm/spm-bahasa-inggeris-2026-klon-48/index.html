Pratonton Contoh Soalan Klon & Skema Jawapan (KLON 48)

Berikut ialah 3 contoh petikan soalan daripada kertas klon ini. Buka mod interaktif penuh untuk menjawab keseluruhan set lengkap pemarkahan pintar:

Contoh 1 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Part 1: Short Texts (Email) [1 Markah]

To: Aiman From: Sarah Subject: Photography Workshop Hi Aiman, I saw an ad for a weekend photography workshop. It&rsquo;s a bit pricey, but the instructor is a professional National Geographic photographer. If we sign up together, we get a 20% group discount. Let me know by Friday if you&rsquo;re interested so I can book our slots! — From the email, we know that Sarah wants to

  • A. attend a photography workshop on Friday.
  • B. save money by booking the workshop with a friend.
  • C. invite a professional photographer to the photography workshop.
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 1
Jawapan Skema: B. save money by booking the workshop with a friend.
Huraian: Sarah states, 'If we sign up together, we get a 20% group discount,' showing she wants to save money by registering together with Aiman (Friday is only the deadline to reply, while the workshop is on the weekend).
Contoh 2 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Part 1: Short Texts (Public Warning) [1 Markah]

CITY HEATWAVE WARNING! A severe heatwave is expected to hit the Eastern region next week. Temperatures are predicted to rise above 40&deg;C. Health officials advise citizens to stay hydrated. Avoid outdoor activities between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM. — Based on the poster, we should

  • A. drink plenty of water during the afternoon.
  • B. exercise outdoors during the hottest part of the day.
  • C. prepare for a period of extremely hot weather.
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 2
Jawapan Skema: C. prepare for a period of extremely hot weather.
Huraian: The warning announces an upcoming severe heatwave next week with temperatures rising above 40°C, advising citizens to stay hydrated and avoid midday outdoor activities — i.e., prepare for extremely hot weather.
Contoh 3 • Kertas 2 (Struktur / Subjektif) — Paper 2 Part 1: Short Communicative Message (Email) [20 Markah]

You must answer this question. You received an email from your friend, Sam , who could not attend your birthday party because he was sick. From: Sam <sam@ymail.com> Subject: Your Birthday Party Hi, I&rsquo;m really sorry I couldn&rsquo;t come to your birthday party last weekend. I&rsquo;m sure everyone had a great time! I would love to hear all about it. What activities did you do with your... — Write an email to Sam ( To: sam@ymail.com | Subject: My Birthday Celebration ) covering all 3 tasks: (1) activities with friends, (2) how many people came, and (3) favourite present & who gave it.

Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 3
Jawapan Skema: Sample Band 5 Email (84 words): To: sam@ymail.com Subject: My Birthday Celebration Hi Sam, I hope you are feeling much better now! We really missed having you at my party last Saturday. About fifteen of our classmates and close cousins came over to celebrate. After cutting the chocolate cake, we played board games, sang karaoke, and enjoyed a backyard barbecue together. My favourite present was a sleek digital watch given by my older brother because it helps me track my daily runs. Let&rsquo;s catch up once you recover! Your friend, Aiman
Huraian: 1119/2 PART 1 ASSESSMENT SCALE (20 Marks = 4 Criteria x 5 Marks): • Content (5m): All 3 required points fully addressed — (1) activities done with friends, (2) number of people who attended, (3) favourite present AND who gave it. • Communicative Achievement (5m): Friendly, natural informal email register suitable for a close friend. • Organisation (5m): Clear greeting, logical paragraphing with cohesive devices, and sign-off. • Language (5m): Accurate past tense verbs and appropriate everyday vocabulary with good grammatical control.
