Pratonton Contoh Soalan Klon & Skema Jawapan (KLON 94)

Berikut ialah 3 contoh petikan soalan daripada set ini. Buka mod interaktif penuh untuk menjawab keseluruhan set lengkap pemarkahan pintar:

Contoh 1 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Part 1: Short Texts [1 Markah]

Read the message from Amara to Ashley about dental clinics: 'Bright Smile Dental Clinic has great reviews and uses the latest technology... Healthy Teeth Centre is known for their friendly staff... City Dental Care offers good prices.' The clinic that offers advanced equipment is:

  • A. Healthy Teeth Center
  • B. Bright Smile Dental Clinic
  • C. City Dental Care
  • D. Amara Dental Specialist
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 1
Jawapan Skema: B. Bright Smile Dental Clinic
Huraian: Amara states that 'Bright Smile Dental Clinic has great reviews and uses the latest technology'.
Contoh 2 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Part 1: Short Texts [1 Markah]

Read the school notice on World Environment Day: 'The Nature Club will monitor classrooms daily and award points to classes that consistently practise energy-saving habits.' From the announcement, we know that the school wants to:

  • A. reward the Nature Club for their environmental efforts
  • B. check whether classes are using eco-friendly products regularly
  • C. replace all classroom air conditioners with fans
  • D. encourage pupils to reduce electricity wastage in classrooms
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 2
Jawapan Skema: D. encourage pupils to reduce electricity wastage in classrooms
Huraian: Switching off lights, fans and air conditioners when not in use encourages pupils to reduce electricity wastage.

Article • Digital and Cash Payments (Questions 27 to 32) Six sentences have been removed from the article. Choose from the sentences A to H the one which fits each gap ( 27 to 32 ). There are two extra sentences which you do not need to use. Digital payment is reshaping how students manage their daily expenses. Since smartphones are prohibited during school hours, cash cards have become an... — Question 27 — Choose the sentence (A–H) that fits Gap 27:

Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 3
Jawapan Skema: C — Furthermore, students do not waste their short recess waiting in line for their change.
Huraian: Official SBP 2026 Answer Key: Q27 = C (1 mark). Connects "reduces long canteen queues" with "saves precious break time" via not waiting in line for change.
