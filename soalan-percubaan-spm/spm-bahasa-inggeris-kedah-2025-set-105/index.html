Pratonton Contoh Soalan & Skema Jawapan (SET 105)

Berikut ialah 3 contoh petikan soalan sebenar daripada kertas ini. Buka mod interaktif penuh untuk menjawab keseluruhan soalan lengkap rajah asal PDF dan pemarkahan pintar:

Contoh 1 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Part 1: Short Texts — Legend of Puteri Zaleha [1 Markah]

"Puteri Zaleha, the daughter of Sultan Sulaiman of Kedah, was known for her extraordinary white blood. When Sultan Iskandar Muda of Acheh wanted to marry her, her father hid her underground." What made Puteri Zaleha special?

  • A. A. She had different blood, unlike others.
  • B. B. She could disappear into the darkness when hiding.
  • C. C. She was hidden underground because she was a princess.
  • D. D. She commanded the soldiers of Acheh.
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 1
Jawapan Skema: A. A. She had different blood, unlike others.
Huraian: According to Kedah Trial 2025 Paper 1 Q1: The text explicitly states she 'was known for her extraordinary white blood', which means she had different blood, unlike others (A).
Contoh 2 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Part 1: Short Texts — Email from Qing Wei [1 Markah]

"Remember the injured stray dog I found near a shelter beside my school last month? His name is Max now! We've adopted him, and he's the sweetest. He's getting better, and he follows me everywhere." What can we understand about Max from the message?

  • A. A. He is a trained pet from a shelter.
  • B. B. He was injured but is now improving.
  • C. C. He was adopted from a veterinary clinic.
  • D. D. He still lives near the school shelter.
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 2
Jawapan Skema: B. B. He was injured but is now improving.
Huraian: According to Kedah Trial 2025 Paper 1 Q2: Max was an injured stray dog and 'He's getting better', meaning he was injured but is now improving (B).
Contoh 3 • Kertas 2 (Struktur / Subjektif) — Paper 2 Part 1 — Short Communicative Message: Going Camping (20 Marks) [20 Markah]

Question 1 (Part 1 — 20 Marks): Read the email from your friend, Ikram, in the diagram below and write a reply of about 80 words addressing all three required content points: — Write your email reply to Ikram (To: ikram@gmail.com, Subject: Going Camping) covering: (1) what items he must bring, (2) where he can get them, and (3) tips to make his camping trip safe [20m].

Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 3
Jawapan Skema: Hi Ikram, I'm so excited to hear about your camping trip next month! First, you must pack a waterproof tent, a warm sleeping bag, a bright torchlight with extra batteries, insect repellent, and a basic first-aid kit (7m). You don't need to spend a fortune—you can easily buy or rent these essentials at reasonable prices from Decathlon or the outdoor camping store in town (6m). To stay safe, pitch your tent on flat ground away from riverbanks, extinguish your campfire completely before sleeping, and stick together with your group at all times. Have fun! Best wishes, Amir (7m).
Huraian: CEFR Marking Scheme (C: 5, CA: 5, O: 5, L: 5 = 20 marks): All 3 content points addressed clearly in an informal email tone with cohesive linkers.
