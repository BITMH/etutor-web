Pratonton Contoh Soalan & Skema Jawapan (SET 60)

Berikut ialah 3 contoh petikan soalan sebenar berserta skema jawapan. Buka mod interaktif penuh untuk menjawab keseluruhan soalan lengkap rajah asal:

Contoh 1 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Part 1: Short Text — Travel Notice [1 Markah]

Notice to Travellers: During the upcoming school holidays, Pulau Redang ferry terminals are expected to be heavily packed, and last-minute resort bookings are almost impossible to secure. Book at least a month in advance! — What does the notice warn travellers about?

  • A. Ferries to the island have been permanently cancelled.
  • B. The island will be crowded and hotel rooms will be hard to get.
  • C. Hotel rates are cheaper for walk-in guests.
  • D. Only students are allowed to board the ferry.
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 1
Jawapan Skema: B. The island will be crowded and hotel rooms will be hard to get.
Huraian: The notice warns that the island will be crowded and hotel rooms will be hard to get without early booking.
Contoh 2 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Part 1: Short Text — Personal Message [1 Markah]

Hi Danish, Thanks for coming to watch my debate finals yesterday! Seeing you and the team cheering from the front row really boosted my confidence on stage. — Amir — Why did Amir write to Danish?

  • A. To invite Danish to join the school debate club.
  • B. To apologise for losing the debate competition.
  • C. To thank Danish because his presence made him feel important and supported.
  • D. To ask Danish for notes on the debate topic.
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 2
Jawapan Skema: C. To thank Danish because his presence made him feel important and supported.
Huraian: Amir thanked Danish because his support made him feel important and confident on stage.
Contoh 3 • Kertas 2 (Struktur / Subjektif) — Part 1: Short Communicative Message — Email on 'Paper Bags' [20 Markah]

From: liana@email.com Subject: Paper Bags Hi, The supermarket near my house has stopped giving plastic bags. Now, shoppers have to pay 50 sen for a paper bag or bring their own reusable bag. Which one do you think is better, and why? Also, how can I reuse or recycle the old paper bags I already have at home? Write back soon! Liana — Write your answer. (a) State your preference (bringing your own reusable bag vs paying 50 sen for a paper bag) and explain WHY. [10 marks] (b) Suggest practical ways Liana can reuse or recycle her old paper bags at home (write a complete email of about 80 words). [10 marks]

Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 3
Jawapan Skema: To: liana@email.com Subject: Re: Paper Bags Hi Liana, It is great to hear from you! In my opinion, bringing your own reusable woven or canvas bag is definitely the better choice. Firstly, it helps you save 50 sen every time you shop, and reusable bags are much stronger and washable compared to paper bags that tear easily when carrying wet groceries. As for the old paper bags you have at home, you can cut them up to wrap your school textbooks or birthday gifts. You can also use them to store dry recyclables like old newspapers before sending them to the recycling centre. Hope this helps! Best wishes, [Candidate]
Huraian: CEFR B1 Assessment: Content (5m — all 3 prompts addressed) + Communicative Achievement (5m — friendly informal email register) + Organisation (5m — clear paragraphing & cohesive devices) + Language (5m — accurate vocabulary & grammar) = 20m.
