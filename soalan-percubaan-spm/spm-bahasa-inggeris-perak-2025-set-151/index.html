Pratonton Contoh Soalan & Skema Jawapan (SET 151)

Berikut ialah 3 contoh petikan soalan sebenar berserta skema jawapan. Buka mod interaktif penuh untuk menjawab keseluruhan soalan lengkap rajah asal:

Contoh 1 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Bahasa Inggeris (1119/1 & 1119/2) [1 Markah]

Read the email from Deena to Nor carefully. What is Deena's purpose of writing this email?

  • A. To suggest Nor to use other languages.
  • B. To remind Nor that she has to be perfect and be happy.
  • C. To advise Nor to trust herself and make the most of everything.
  • D. To ask Nor to cancel her speech presentation.
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 1
Jawapan Skema: C. To advise Nor to trust herself and make the most of everything.
Huraian: Deena encourages Nor to be confident, trust herself, and enjoy the experience ('the important thing is to be confident and enjoy the experience').
Contoh 2 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Bahasa Inggeris (1119/1 & 1119/2) [1 Markah]

Read the Urgent Notice for SMK Juara students. From the notice, we know that a clean classroom:

  • A. must have more space.
  • B. has clean floors and windows only.
  • C. encourages attention and fun learning.
  • D. requires professional cleaners every day.
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 2
Jawapan Skema: C. encourages attention and fun learning.
Huraian: The notice states: 'A clean environment helps everyone focus better and creates a more pleasant atmosphere for learning' (C).
Contoh 3 • Kertas 2 (Struktur / Subjektif) — Paper 1 Part 3: Reading Comprehension (Alex and Daniel's Friendship & Football Tournament) [3 Markah]

Paper 1 Part 3: Reading Comprehension (Alex and Daniel's Friendship & Football Tournament) — (a) Based on paragraphs 1 and 2, why was Alex sitting alone in the cafeteria on the first day of school, and how did he and Daniel become friends?

Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 3
Jawapan Skema: Alex was sitting alone because he had recently moved to a new town, was unfamiliar with his surroundings, and did not know anyone yet. He and Daniel became friends when Daniel noticed Alex sitting alone, greeted him warmly, and invited Alex to join his group at a nearby table.
Huraian: - Alex was a newcomer / unfamiliar with his surroundings and knew no one [1m] - Daniel noticed him sitting alone and warmly invited him to join his table [2m]
