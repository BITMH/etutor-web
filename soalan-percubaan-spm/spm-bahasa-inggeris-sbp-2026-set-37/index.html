Pratonton Contoh Soalan & Skema Jawapan (SET 37)

Berikut ialah 3 contoh petikan soalan sebenar daripada kertas ini. Buka mod interaktif penuh untuk menjawab keseluruhan soalan lengkap rajah asal PDF dan pemarkahan pintar:

Contoh 1 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Part 1: Short Texts (Advertisement) [1 Markah]

Based on the advertisement, customers will pay a lower price if they

  • A. provide their own food.
  • B. make an early reservation.
  • C. prepare their own equipment.
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 1
Jawapan Skema: B. make an early reservation.
Huraian: Official SBP 2026 Answer: B . The advertisement states, "Book by the 15th June to enjoy a special discount!" — booking ahead means making an early reservation (paying a lower price). All meals are already included (not A), and bringing hiking boots/insect repellent is a safety requirement, not a discount condition (not C).
Contoh 2 • Kertas 1 (Objektif) — Part 1: Short Texts (Dialogue) [1 Markah]

Which aspect is seen as a concern of the new X-Phone 17?

  • A. Its selling cost.
  • B. Its photo resolution.
  • C. Its energy efficiency.
Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 2
Jawapan Skema: C. Its energy efficiency.
Huraian: Official SBP 2026 Answer: C . Mia says, "the battery life is very disappointing and drains too quickly even with light use," and Zack agrees. Poor battery life that drains rapidly refers to its energy efficiency . Its price is affordable (not A) and its camera quality is outstanding (not B).
Contoh 3 • Kertas 2 (Struktur / Subjektif) — Part 4: Digital and Cash Payments at School (Gapped Text A–H) [1 Markah]

Article • Digital and Cash Payments (Questions 27 to 32) Six sentences have been removed from the article. Choose from the sentences A to H the one which fits each gap ( 27 to 32 ). There are two extra sentences which you do not need to use. — Question 27 — Choose the sentence (A–H) that fits Gap 27:

Semak Skema Jawapan & Ulasan Contoh 3
Jawapan Skema: C — Furthermore, students do not waste their short recess waiting in line for their change.
Huraian: Official SBP 2026 Answer Key: Q27 = C (1 mark). Connects "reduces long canteen queues" with "saves precious break time" via not waiting in line for change.
